## Purpose

An automated biotechnology plant that manufactures weapons and synthetic bodies resembling crew templates without proving where or how the crew originated.

| Layer | Biome role |
|---|---|
| Material intake / transport | Installed rail, loading infrastructure and active logistics establish a plant still operating without ordinary human supervision. |
| Assembly / weapons production | Work machines perform useful industrial functions that become threats when the crew intrudes. |
| Synthetic-body manufacture | Assembly cradles and unfinished bodies make a manufactured origin plausible without certifying that these are the crew's sources. |
| Inspection / security | Production monitoring and access control share an institutional machine language rather than an occupying gang's improvised defences. |
| Isolated research / recovered system | Research spaces can be separated from the line; recovering a crew member does not require destroying the system used later. |

This is a world-structure proposal, not an accepted art palette, hazard list, hostile policy, complete factory map or claim about who owns Helix. Rails connect the setting to [[Gameplay/Rail Network|the existing network]] without explaining its wider authority.

## Evidence boundary

> **Accepted — Mystery role:** Helix raises the question of whether the crew's bodies were manufactured. Its production evidence is suggestive, not proof of origin, replacement, copying or reconstruction.

Do not turn unfinished bodies into a demonstration of Coffin recovery or use naming/serial labels to resolve the identity question. [[Missions/M04#evidence-and-communication|M04]] owns its specific evidence beat; [[Missions/M05|M05]] owns subsequent compatibility revelations.
