## Purpose

A subterranean biological archive where the Continuum claims to preserve distinct identities that institutional systems discarded or erased.

## Narrative evidence

| Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Distinguishable voices identify themselves as related to earlier crew profiles. | Their testimony does not establish biological continuity or an objective crew origin. |
| Organic systems retain memories across bodies and environments. | Preservation does not prove those memories originally belonged to one person. |
| The Continuum frames coexistence as protection from replacement and erasure. | Its account may be sincere without being complete or neutral. |
| Records imply institutional disposal or suppression. | Do not establish that OPERATOR personally ordered, performed, or understood those acts. |

B99 culminates in [[Missions/SE01|SE01]], where Communion requires an explicit accept-or-return choice.
