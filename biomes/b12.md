## Controller boundary

Controller coordinates the current experiment and connected facilities. OPERATOR-authenticated traffic crosses its infrastructure, but the relationship between OPERATOR and Controller remains unresolved. The physical boss is a replaceable local avatar, not proof of Controller's complete embodiment.
