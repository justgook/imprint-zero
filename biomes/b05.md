## Purpose

A biological exclusion zone where the [[NPCs/Continuum|Continuum]] presents collective preservation as an alternative to institutional replacement and erasure.

## Memory-scene visual and audio language

> **Accepted** — B05's linked Specialization memories interweave biological growth with exclusion-zone infrastructure: damp surfaces, root-threaded machinery, and muffled ventilation. The growth is not identified as hostile or benevolent, and these connections do not establish where the remembered events occurred.
