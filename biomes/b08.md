## Dominant question

> If bodies and memories are reused, what—if anything—continues from one person to the next?

## Production model

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    Remains[Utilized bodies<br/>and human remains] --> Cradle[Cradle assembly]
    Zero[Imprint Zero<br/>reference patterns] --> Cradle
    Records[Returned Mission records<br/>adaptations + selected memories] --> Cradle
    Cradle --> Crews[Later Zero Division crews]
    Records --> One[Imprint One<br/>parallel replacement project]
```

The Cradle repairs viable organs and tissue, grows missing material, and uses the remainder as nutrients and biological feedstock. The process cannot identify which physical or mnemonic pieces persist into a particular Character.
