## Purpose

A cathedral-like memory archive that asks how identities can be extracted, edited, combined, and weaponized without proving how the crew originated.

## Physical wire-web language

> **Working version — Familiar mechanism, alien purpose:** Memories and emotions are unfamiliar storage material; their presentation uses familiar physical media, readers, plugs, relays and cabling. The archive's connections form literal spider-web-like arrangements of wires: radial anchor runs, finer cross-connections, tensioned spans and dense junctions around storage/processing nodes. This is retro-futurist cyberpunk hardware, not biological silk, magical threads or a holographic network.

The web is operating infrastructure, not evidence that the Basilica is abandoned. Helpers visibly lay, tension, connect and repair it. Storage surfaces and access clearances suit task-adapted inhabitants rather than human comfort; human traversal occupies service gaps through a space built for other bodies. Keep the required playable route ordinary-kit accessible and visually readable despite that alien ergonomics.

The Curator and its helpers share a spider-like structural language adapted to their jobs, not a confirmed biological species or religious origin. The familiar web/body analogy does not prove consciousness, manufacture, memory provenance or who controls the wider experiment network. Physical cables are not themselves the global Mesh or evidence of OPERATOR's identity.

### Weaver role — connection-based defence

**Weaver** is a working role/name for a helper that installs and services the wire-web, then redirects that same capability into local defence when humans intrude. Its ordinary work should remain visible outside combat. It is not yet an allocated Enemy ID, a new M05 preview archetype, or an authored placement in MA01.

> **Working version — Connect, then defend:** The Weaver's first defensive role uses connected machinery, not laid traps or a generic bite/leap. It establishes one visible local connection to an already-present compatible guard or authored security endpoint, then coordinates that endpoint's ordinary defensive cycle. The player chooses to interrupt the exposed connector/body or evade and engage the endpoint. Connection wind-up and endpoint commitment remain readable; no instant shot, shortened hidden tell or universal shielding is implied.

Outside combat, spool/anchor/gripper work explains the same anatomy and connections. Its physical cable is a local authored relationship, not a network-wide buff, unlimited ally spawning, killed-enemy reconstruction or proof that every wire can be cut. Baseline attacks must be sufficient to interrupt the reachable connector/body; Hack may supply an authored alternative later, never the only counter.
