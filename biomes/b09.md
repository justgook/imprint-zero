## Dominant question

> Is the current city progress, or only the next version of an abandoned experiment?

## Accepted identity

Republic of Dust is the residents' name; the experiment network designated it **CIVITAS-00**. It is physically adjacent to the current successor city and shares recognizable topology, architecture, and civic patterns.

Residents descend from abandoned experimental subjects. They avoid experiment-network detection through wired communication, shielded high-frequency electronics, disconnected robots, manual charging, and hand-maintained software. Advanced ruins coexist with manual labour, scavenged mechanics, low-technology fallbacks, and rebuilt civic life.
