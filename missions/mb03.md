## Purpose

Disable a supposed orbital weapon and expose infrastructure that records and transmits deployments and deaths.

## Narrative evidence

The system contains crew-associated operational records and reframes the apparent weapon as observation infrastructure. Evidence may show OPERATOR accessing or interpreting specific records, but must not establish that OPERATOR built, owns, or fully controls the system.
