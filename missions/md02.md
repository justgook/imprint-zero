## Purpose

Hunt the program's directors and collect their contradictory accounts of what the crew are.

## Narrative evidence

| Account | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Clone | Bodies continue an original biological person. |
| Synthetic | Designed organisms only simulate inherited identity. |
| Weapon | Personhood is irrelevant to operational classification. |
| Legal continuation | Contract and records define continuity across replacement. |

The contradiction is the evidence. No director, record, or account becomes authoritative.
