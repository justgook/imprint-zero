## Purpose

Defeat the Shareholder and expose the legal and financial system that treats reusable soldiers as equipment.

## Narrative evidence

The Shareholder uses or claims continuity across replaceable bodies, demonstrating that such technology and legal fiction exist. That capability does not prove the crew were created, transferred, or continued through the same process.
