## Imprint Zero

Four altered bodies awaken sequentially. Each corresponds to one crew identity, uses recognizable capabilities, and may expose one ability the player has not unlocked.

They are biological and mnemonic reference templates used by the Cradle. M09 does not explain what **Imprint Zero** means or establish whether the bodies are original humans, early copies, modified donors, composites, or replacements that inherited an older designation.

## Imprint One

Imprint One is a long-running prototype intended to replace a four-Character crew with one deployable body. Returned operational records repeatedly updated it, including the current crew's latest capabilities, but integration failed to produce one stable identity.

Premature activation under a termination directive produces personality, memory, skill, and combat shifts that read as malfunctions rather than communicating people. Mismatches between active identity and capability create attack openings. Imprint One demonstrates no stable self, reflective agency, or capacity to stop, though the game cannot objectively disprove inner experience. The crew kills it as an uncontrolled threat.

## Standard ending

After the fight, the crew deliberately overloads the production core rather than causing an automatic boss explosion. The active Character disables production-line safeties, RELAY redirects stored fabrication energy, and the active Character triggers the irreversible overload at extraction. The full crew escapes and watches the Cradle collapse from a distant overlook.

The ending stops the immediate replacement cycle without proving the crew's origin, the meaning of Imprint Zero, OPERATOR's identity, or the purpose of the wider production network.
