## Biomes

* B99 — The Living Archive

## Narrative Function

The Communion route is an optional early ending hidden within B05.

It reveals part of the cloning truth before the crew reaches the Cradle and allows the protagonist to join the Continuum, a distributed biological consciousness that preserves distinct identities rather than erasing them.

This route answers one question early:

> Can several lives continue as one without being erased?

It does not reveal the full truth about OPERATOR, the world outside the city, or the ultimate purpose of Imprint Zero.

The route should feel like a valid ending rather than a failure, while still encouraging another campaign.

---

## Entry Conditions

B99 is hidden within Verdant Null.

The entrance is not a technological door. It is a living passage that appears only after the player demonstrates that they will not automatically follow OPERATOR’s extermination orders.

Possible requirements include:

* Preserve at least two growth-nodes in MC01 — Burn the Garden.
* Listen to all source-memory voices in MC02 — Voices Under Glass.
* Refuse OPERATOR’s purge command in MC03 — Heartroot.
* Carry a corrupted or unclassified Imprint.
* Locate the root-shaped symbol in MS02 — Beneath the Skin.

Completing the required conditions reveals the passage into B99.

---

## Crew State

The Communion route can occur before the crew understands the full Imprint Zero program.

At this point:

* The crew knows that its memories and bodies may have been manufactured.
* Previous versions of Zero Division have been discovered or implied.
* OPERATOR remains the official mission commander.
* The Continuum has shown that shared consciousness does not require the destruction of individuality.
* The protagonist must choose whether forbidden knowledge is enough, or whether joining the archive is a more meaningful form of survival.

Because this is an early ending, the crew’s understanding remains incomplete.

---

## OPERATOR Relationship

OPERATOR describes the Continuum as a hostile hive mind and treats entry into B99 as contamination, capture, or mission failure.

The Living Archive contradicts that description. It preserves multiple identities without reducing them to one controlling personality.

OPERATOR’s opposition should remain understandable: joining the Continuum removes the protagonist from its mission structure and makes erased crew iterations impossible to suppress.

---

## Progression Function

The route builds on the organic and adaptive progression introduced in B05.

Possible rewards before the final choice include:

* Organic movement technology
* Regeneration or adaptive mutation
* Access to preserved crew memories
* Forbidden knowledge about previous iterations
* Communion ending access

Choosing to return instead of ending the campaign may allow the protagonist to carry some of this knowledge back into the standard route.

# B99 — Preserved Collective Identity

## Working Name

**B99: The Living Archive**

Alternative title:

**B99: Eden Below**

## Environment

A vast subterranean ecosystem built around abandoned cloning chambers.

Roots grow through thousands of glass tanks. Animals carry fragments of human memory. Waterways pulse like neural pathways. Dead soldiers speak through flowers, insects, and fungal networks.

The environment should feel unsettling but not malicious.

Pixel-art palette:

* Deep forest black
* Soft cyan
* Warm gold
* Translucent green
* Memory-white

## Narrative Purpose

B99 reveals that the Continuum rescued discarded clones from the Cradle’s disposal systems.

The bodies of previous crew iterations are dead, but portions of their consciousness survive in the network. The Continuum does not know which memories were real; it has simply preserved every version OPERATOR attempted to erase.

The biome reframes collective identity as preservation and coexistence rather than assimilation.

---

## Missions

### SE01 — Become Many

The protagonist enters the deepest Continuum node.

There, they encounter previous iterations of the crew speaking through the Living Archive. These identities remain distinguishable even while sharing one biological network.

The Continuum offers the protagonist a choice:

* Return to the campaign carrying forbidden knowledge.
* Free the archived personalities.
* Join the Continuum.
* Destroy it to preserve individual identity.

### Communion Ending

If the protagonist joins the Continuum, their sprite dissolves into roots and light, initially suggesting death. The surrounding environment then begins moving with their gestures.

Voices from former crew iterations speak without merging into a single voice.

The final message could be:

> I was never the person I remembered.
>
> I was never only one person.
>
> But I was here.
>
> And now, nothing they erased is lost.

The ending confirms that the protagonist continues within the network. It reveals the cloning program without resolving the deeper mysteries reserved for the standard and true-ending routes.

---

## End-State of the Route

By the end of Communion:

* The protagonist has rejected OPERATOR’s definition of individual continuity.
* Previous crew iterations remain preserved rather than erased.
* The Continuum gains a willing participant instead of absorbing a victim.
* The wider campaign ends before OPERATOR and the Cradle are fully understood.
* Preserved collective identity becomes an alternative to both replacement and permanent isolation.
