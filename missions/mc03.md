## Purpose

Reach the Continuum's central organism and choose whether to destroy it, extract it, or accept its mark.

## Narrative evidence

[[Bosses/Heartroot|Heartroot]] presents destruction, extraction, and marked coexistence as competing local responses to collective identity. None is declared philosophically correct, and the mark does not unlock Communion; that route depends only on the MS01 survivor flag revealed during MS03.
