## Progression

HUB1 unlocks after [[Missions/M05|M05]]. The crew returns here after each Act II Mission; completed Missions remain replayable and the next Mission in that biome becomes available. After all four Guardians are defeated, M06 becomes available but does not start automatically. The player may continue Act II until explicitly confirming the point of no return and entering [[Missions/HUB2|HUB2]].
