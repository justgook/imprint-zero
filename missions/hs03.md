Defeat Controller's local avatar, sever the current city from the experiment network, and complete the extended route without proving OPERATOR's identity or the experiment's full scale.

## Controller encounter

Controller is a tower-wide system fought through replaceable shells and the environment. Civic-control hardware, surveillance, drones, doors, public-address channels, and false objectives participate.

| Route | Accepted advantage |
|---|---|
| HA | Identify false memories, corrupt behavioural predictions, and force inconsistent decisions. |
| HB | Disable shell transfer, reroute power, and isolate physical infrastructure. |

Either route traps one local avatar long enough to disconnect the city. Destroying it does not prove Controller has no copies elsewhere.

## Institutional revelation

The local system iterates experimental cities, abandoned Republic of Dust after failure, develops controllable minds through B11, develops replaceable bodies through B10, manufactures Zero Division teams through the Cradle, validates them in the successor city, and supplies teams as governance, policing, and suppression assets to external clients.

It does not identify every client, reveal the full scale, or prove who originated the program.

## Ending

There is no final choice menu. The network disappears from the current city and residents begin acting independently. An ambiguous montage makes the manually rebuilt city resemble Republic of Dust without confirming whether the images are future, past, mind dive, prediction, or repetition.

After the credits, OPERATOR addresses an unidentified team:

> “Good work, team. Experiment **CIVITAS-01** complete. Proceed to **CIVITAS-02**.”
