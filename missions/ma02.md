## Purpose

Stop a memory trafficker who forcibly extracts emotional and behavioral traits from living civilians and claims the crew's courage, fear, loyalty, and attachments came through the same market.

## Mission framing

| Layer | MA02 direction |
|---|---|
| OPERATOR briefing | Stop an active extraction operation, release captives, prevent distribution, and recover victim records. |
| Immediate benefit | Living victims are freed from ongoing extraction and stolen trait packages are contained. |
| Identity contradiction | The trafficker recognizes traits expressed by the deployed Character and attributes them to alleged source people. |
| Evidence boundary | Predatory extraction is real; the trafficker's crew-origin claims remain commercially motivated and potentially manipulated. |

## Narrative evidence

> **Accepted** — The trafficker recognizes different emotional or behavioral traits in each deployed Character, names different alleged source people, and provides partial supporting records. Replaying MA02 with another Character adds that Character's allegations to the evidence archive; contradictions remain visible rather than being reconciled into a donor map.

The trafficker treats identity as divisible inventory. Recovered supply records identify the Curator as an upstream editor or supplier, unlocking and motivating MA03 without proving the Curator's account of identity. Commercial self-interest, edited records, and altered memory prevent any allegation from becoming objective truth.
