# Imprint Zero — Campaign Shape

## Campaign Overview

The campaign is structured around three main routes:

1. **Standard Route**
   Complete the main campaign and defeat the standard final boss in M09.

2. **Early Secret Route**
   Discover the hidden exit in B05, enter B99, and complete SE01 to receive the first secret ending.

3. **True Ending Route**
   Discover the hidden area connected to B07, complete B09, choose either the HA or HB route, and reunite at B12 for the true final sequence.

---

# ACT I — Crew Assembly

## Biomes

* B01
* B02

## Narrative Function

Act I introduces the four playable crew members one at a time.

The player begins in the middle of an operation, without a long introduction. OPERATOR appears to be the crew’s mission commander, while the characters initially understand themselves as members of an established special-forces unit.

Each mission expands the playable roster and introduces part of the game’s core systems.

---

## M01 — ROOK Deployment

### Playable Character

* ROOK

### Supporting Characters

* OPERATOR communicates over the radio.
* VECTOR is heard over the radio but is not yet playable.

### Function

M01 is the immediate gameplay introduction.

The player is deployed directly into an active mission with little context. The mission teaches ROOK’s basic movement, combat, and tactical abilities.

### Narrative Purpose

* Establish ROOK as the initial point-of-view character.
* Introduce OPERATOR as a reliable mission commander.
* Introduce VECTOR through radio dialogue.
* Establish that the crew has operated together before, even though the player has not met them.
* Avoid explaining the world too early.

### Character State

ROOK begins with the default subclass:

* **TACTICAL**

The name “Tactical” is not initially shown as a subclass name. It is presented simply as ROOK’s normal combat configuration.

---

## Hub Introduction

After M01, the player enters the crew hub for the first time.

The hub introduces:

* ROOK
* VECTOR
* OPERATOR’s mission interface
* Character selection
* Equipment management
* Mission briefings
* Locked crew stations belonging to RAM and RELAY

The hub should initially feel functional and familiar to the characters, even though it is new to the player.

---

## M02 — First Boss

### Playable Characters

The player can choose:

* ROOK
* VECTOR

### Function

M02 introduces character selection and the first major boss encounter.

### Narrative Purpose

* Demonstrate that missions can be approached differently depending on the selected character.
* Establish the differences between ROOK and VECTOR.
* Introduce the first significant mission contradiction or unexplained data fragment.
* Give the player the first indication that OPERATOR may be withholding information.

### Character States

ROOK:

* Tactical

VECTOR:

* Scout

As with ROOK, “Scout” initially appears to be VECTOR’s normal class rather than one of several possible configurations.

---

## M03 — RAM Intervention

### Playable Characters

The player begins as:

* ROOK or VECTOR

### Mission Structure

The player reaches a boss or powerful target but cannot defeat it through normal means.

The fight should feel intentionally unwinnable without becoming a prolonged fake battle.

At the critical moment, RAM enters the mission and breaks through the boss’s defenses.

### Narrative Purpose

* Introduce RAM through action rather than exposition.
* Establish RAM as the crew’s breaching and destructive-force specialist.
* Show that some enemies, routes, and environmental structures require specific crew abilities.
* Begin teaching the player to recognize character-specific obstacles.

### Character State

RAM begins with:

* **BREACHER**

The Breacher name remains hidden until RAM later unlocks another subclass.

---

## M04 — Rescue RELAY

### Playable Character

* RAM

### Mission Premise

RELAY entered a restricted facility to retrieve data.

During the operation, she discovered something unexpected and sealed herself inside part of the facility. The crew initially believes she is trapped, but it becomes clear that she deliberately locked the area to prevent the data from being destroyed, transmitted, or accessed by an unknown party.

### Mission Structure

RAM must force a path through the facility and reach RELAY.

This mission should emphasize:

* Destructible barriers
* Heavy environmental hazards
* Forced entry
* Structural collapse
* Routes that only RAM can open

### Narrative Purpose

* Make RAM fully playable.
* Introduce RELAY as more than a support technician.
* Establish that RELAY may have seen data OPERATOR did not want the crew to access.
* Introduce the idea that mission data can contain Imprints.
* Set up M05.

---

## M05 — The Four Trials

### Primary Playable Character

* RELAY

### Secondary Playable Characters

During each trial, the player switches between RELAY and one other crew member:

* RELAY and ROOK
* RELAY and VECTOR
* RELAY and RAM
* RELAY alone or RELAY interacting with all three crew profiles

### Mission Premise

RELAY attempts to extract, reconstruct, or decrypt the data recovered in M04.

The data is protected by four adaptive trials. Each trial is connected to one member of the crew and responds to that member’s neural profile.

These trials may initially appear to be security simulations, but they are actually testing compatibility between the crew and recovered Imprints.

### Trial Structure

#### Trial 1 — ROOK

Focus:

* Tactical combat
* Positioning
* Command decisions
* Coordinated switching between RELAY and ROOK

#### Trial 2 — VECTOR

Focus:

* Speed
* Scouting
* Precision movement
* Remote system manipulation

#### Trial 3 — RAM

Focus:

* Destruction
* Momentum
* Environmental interaction
* Opening paths for RELAY

#### Trial 4 — RELAY

Focus:

* Network control
* Data-space traversal
* System corruption
* Imprint extraction

### Gameplay Purpose

M05 unlocks a major shared progression system.

Each character receives an Imprint that expands their core mechanics.

Possible unlock categories include:

* Overdrive
* Character-specific secondary action
* Imprint equipment
* Crew switching systems
* Access to restricted mission routes
* New traversal interactions
* Forge or technology access

The exact reward distribution can be decided separately, but M05 should represent the moment when the full game structure opens.

### Narrative Purpose

* Make RELAY playable.
* Complete the initial crew roster.
* Introduce Imprints as both gameplay upgrades and story objects.
* Reveal that the recovered data recognizes the crew at a biological or neural level.
* Suggest that the trials were designed specifically for them.
* Begin the mystery of why the crew’s identities are stored inside external systems.

# ACT II — Open Mission Operations

## Biomes

The open-operation section consists of four selectable biomes.

Based on the original biome table, these are:

* B03
* B04
* B05
* B06

The current description refers to B04–B06, but four selectable locations require B03–B06. Until changed, B03 should remain part of this act.

## Mission Structure

The player may select any of the four biomes in any order.

This section follows a structure similar to games where several locations are available from a central selection screen.

Each biome contains:

* One initially available main mission
* A second mission unlocked by completing the first
* A third mission or optional branch
* In some biomes, a hidden special mission

Completing one mission in a biome expands that biome rather than immediately completing it.

---

## Act II Progression Loop

### Step 1

The player selects one of four available biomes.

### Step 2

The first mission introduces the biome’s faction, hazard, and central objective.

### Step 3

Completing that mission opens:

* The next main mission
* Additional routes
* Character-specific access points
* Possible secret mission conditions

### Step 4

The player may remain in that biome or return to the hub and select another.

### Step 5

Imprints found throughout the four biomes unlock additional subclasses.

# Subclass Structure

Each crew member has three total subclasses.

The first subclass is their default playstyle.

The first subclass name is hidden at the beginning of the game. It becomes visible only after the player unlocks that character’s second subclass.

This reveal communicates that the character’s original abilities were not simply their natural identity. They were already one selectable configuration.

---

## ROOK

### Default

* TACTICAL

### Unlockable

* HEAVY
* ASSAULT

### General Identity

ROOK is the adaptable combat leader.

* Tactical emphasizes control, positioning, and planned combat.
* Heavy emphasizes armor, sustained weapons, and defensive pressure.
* Assault emphasizes direct aggression, mobility, and close-range offense.

---

## VECTOR

### Default

* SCOUT

### Unlockable

* PHASE
* HUNTER

### General Identity

VECTOR is the high-speed mobility and precision specialist.

* Scout emphasizes speed, information, and traversal.
* Phase emphasizes evasion, spatial manipulation, or short-range displacement.
* Hunter emphasizes target tracking, precision damage, and pursuit.

---

## RAM

### Default

* BREACHER

### Unlockable

* SIEGE
* IMPACT

### General Identity

RAM is the force, destruction, and momentum specialist.

* Breacher emphasizes breaking defenses and opening routes.
* Siege emphasizes heavy weapons, area control, and sustained destruction.
* Impact emphasizes momentum, collision attacks, and aggressive movement.

---

## RELAY

### Default

* WIRE

### Unlockable

* NETWORK
* NULL

### General Identity

RELAY is the systems, remote control, and disruption specialist.

* Wire emphasizes direct device interaction and local system control.
* Network emphasizes distributed control, hacking, drones, or linked devices.
* Null emphasizes disabling technology, suppressing enemy abilities, and corrupting systems.

# Act II Subclass Imprints

Each of the four crew members needs two unlockable subclasses.

This creates a total of:

* 8 subclass Imprints

These eight Imprints are distributed across B03–B06.

## Recommended Distribution

Each biome contains two subclass Imprints.

This creates an even structure:

| Biome | Subclass Imprints |
| ----- | ----------------: |
| B03   |                 2 |
| B04   |                 2 |
| B05   |                 2 |
| B06   |                 2 |
| Total |                 8 |

The two Imprints in a biome do not need to belong to the same character.

Mixing them encourages the player to explore all four locations.

# B05 — Early Secret Route

B05 contains the entrance to B99.

This route must be accessible during Act II, before the standard campaign ending.

## Secret-Exit Logic

The player performs a set of optional actions within B05.

Possible requirements include:

* Entering an area with a specific crew member
* Preserving an organism or non-hostile entity
* Recovering a corrupted Imprint
* Ignoring or disobeying an OPERATOR command
* Completing a hidden B05 mission
* Using RELAY’s Null subclass or another late Act II ability
* Revisiting B05 after acquiring an ability from another biome

Completing the requirements reveals the hidden exit.

# B99 — Early Secret Ending

## Mission

* SE01

## Route Function

B99 interrupts the normal campaign.

It reveals the cloning truth earlier than the other routes and leads directly to the first secret ending.

## Core Revelation

The crew members are clones, printed bodies, or repeated biological versions of people who existed before them.

The exact nature of their memories may remain unresolved.

Possible interpretations remain:

* Memories copied from the original people
* Memories assembled from several soldiers
* Memories edited by OPERATOR
* Memories generated to produce stable personalities

## Ending Concept

The active character connects with a biological collective intelligence and becomes part of a larger natural system.

The ending can be presented as peaceful while remaining disturbing.

The character escapes OPERATOR and the cycle of repeated deployment, but loses or surrenders part of their individual existence.

## Ending Tone

This should function as a bad or ambiguous ending rather than the true resolution.

The character learns one truth:

* They are not the original human they believed themselves to be.

However, they do not learn:

* OPERATOR’s full purpose
* Why the crew was created
* What happened outside the operation zones
* Whether individual identity can survive
* Whether the biological collective is genuinely benevolent

## Current Working Outcome

> The character rejects artificial military identity but escapes into another form of control: collective biological identity.

This creates thematic symmetry.

OPERATOR removes freedom through command and repetition.

The biological collective removes freedom through connection and dissolution.

# ACT III — Standard Campaign Route

## Biomes

* B07
* B08

## Missions

Based on the current mission table:

### B07

* M06
* M07

### B08

* M08
* M09

## Narrative Function

Act III begins after the player completes the required Act II objectives.

The campaign becomes linear again.

The crew moves from carrying out missions to directly confronting the system behind them.

---

## B07 — Transition Toward the Truth

B07 should:

* Reframe previous missions
* Reveal inconsistencies in OPERATOR’s story
* Show evidence of previous crew iterations
* Place the crew in direct conflict with OPERATOR or its agents
* Contain the entrance or trigger for B09

The B09 secret route must become available before the player fully commits to the standard ending.

---

## B08 — Standard Finale

B08 contains:

* M08
* M09

### M08

The crew reaches the final operational center, command system, production facility, or source of the apparent crisis.

M08 prepares the final confrontation and confirms part of the crew’s manufactured nature.

### M09 — Standard Final Boss

M09 is the final boss of the standard route.

Completing M09 produces the standard ending.

## Standard Ending Function

The standard ending should resolve the immediate campaign but provide only an incomplete version of the truth.

Possible standard-ending revelations:

* The crew are manufactured soldiers.
* OPERATOR has deployed multiple versions of them.
* Their memories have been edited.
* Their identities may be composites rather than direct copies.

The standard ending does not fully reveal:

* The origin of the Imprint program
* The larger world outside OPERATOR’s control
* The full purpose of the repeated campaigns
* The meaning of Imprint Zero
* Whether the crew can define themselves independently

The standard ending should feel complete enough for a first playthrough while clearly leaving deeper questions open.

# ACT IV — True Ending Route

## Entry Point

The true-ending route begins through a secret discovered in B07.

The player must access this secret before moving too far into the B08 finale or before completing M09.

## First True-Route Biome

* B09

## B09 Function

B09 acts as the gateway to the hidden campaign.

It confirms that the standard mission structure is incomplete or deliberately fabricated.

B09 contains:

* HS01
* HB01

The exact role of HB01 may need adjustment because the later route also uses the HB mission prefix. It can remain as an early HB-route setup mission or be renamed for clarity.

# Route Split

After B09, the player chooses one of two paths:

* HA route
* HB route

The routes reveal different aspects of the truth.

Neither path alone provides the entire answer.

Both eventually lead to B12.

---

* Original genetic donors

* Memory selection
* Composite personalities
* Failed crew versions
* Whether an “original” person still exists

# B12 — True Finale

## Missions

* HS02
* HS03

## Entry

Both HA and HB ultimately lead to B12.

The player reaches B12 with different information depending on the selected route.

The routes may provide different:

* Dialogue
* Boss phases
* Imprint options
* Crew decisions
* Final abilities
* Interpretations of the truth

## HS02 — Convergence

HS02 reunites the two possible narrative paths.

Even though the player only completed HA or HB during the current playthrough, the mission should provide enough evidence to understand the missing side.

Another possibility is that the selected route determines which truth remains uncertain.

### Function

* Prepare the true final confrontation.
* Resolve remaining crew conflicts.
* Define the player’s position on identity.
* Determine which Imprints are accepted, removed, or combined.
* Establish the final version of each crew member.

---

## HS03 — True Final Boss

HS03 is the true final boss and final narrative decision.

The boss should represent more than OPERATOR as an individual machine.

It should represent the system that treats identity as editable military equipment.

Possible final boss forms include:

* OPERATOR using perfected copies of the crew
* A merged command intelligence assembled from all failed iterations
* A previous crew that accepted OPERATOR’s control
* A system that continually rewrites the arena and the characters
* An idealized “complete” Imprint Zero body

## True Ending Function

The true ending answers the central questions:

* What are the crew members?
* How were their memories created?
* Why were they deployed repeatedly?
* What is OPERATOR trying to achieve?
* Does an artificial or composite origin invalidate personhood?

The emotional conclusion should be:

> Their memories may not be entirely theirs, but the people formed by those memories are real.

# Points That Remain Open

## 1. B03 Inclusion

The campaign requires four selectable Act II locations.

The original table provides B03, B04, B05, and B06, while the newer description says B04–B06.

The current cleaned structure assumes:

* B03–B06 are the four selectable biomes.

## 2. M05 Unlock Distribution

M05 clearly unlocks new mechanics for all four crew members.

The exact rewards still need to be assigned, such as:

* Overdrive
* Forge
* Imprint equipment
* Character switching
* Technology access
* Specialized traversal

## 3. Eight Subclass Imprint Locations

The eight unlockable subclasses must be assigned to specific missions and character-locked rooms across B03–B06.

## 4. B05 Collective

The biological intelligence still needs:

* A final name
* Clear rules
* A visual language
* A reason it preserves clones
* A cost for joining it
* A relationship to the original Imprint technology

## 5. Standard Ending Truth

The exact amount revealed after M09 needs to be defined so that it is satisfying but does not invalidate the true route.

## 6. HA and HB Route Choice

It remains to be decided whether:

* The player permanently chooses one route per playthrough.
* One route becomes accessible after completing the other.
* New Game Plus allows the player to combine evidence from both.
* Both routes are required for the fullest variation of the true ending.

## 7. HB01 Placement

HB01 currently appears in B09, while HB02–HB04 appear in B11.

This can work if HB01 is the introduction to the HB route. Otherwise, it should receive a neutral or HS mission code.
