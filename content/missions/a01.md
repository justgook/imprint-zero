## Biomes

* B01 — Ashfall District
* B02 — Helix Foundry

## Narrative Function

Act I introduces the four playable crew members one at a time while establishing the first mystery surrounding Zero Division, Imprints, and the crew’s identities.

The player begins in the middle of an active operation without a long introduction. ROOK, VECTOR, RAM, and RELAY already know one another and consider themselves members of an established special-forces unit, but the player initially sees only part of that unit.

OPERATOR appears to be the crew’s trusted mission commander. OPERATOR is calm, competent, and protective throughout Act I. Although some mission details are unclear or contradictory, the crew does not yet seriously suspect OPERATOR of deception.

Each mission introduces a new character, system, or type of character-specific interaction.

Character selection occurs only in the HUB before a mission.

The only exception is M05, where character changes are scripted as part of the mission’s trial structure.

# B01 — The Neon Quarantine

## Working Name

**B01: Ashfall District**

## Environment

A densely packed cyberpunk residential sector under permanent acid rain.

Neon signs flicker behind quarantine plastic. Emergency lights illuminate abandoned apartments, flooded streets, medical checkpoints, and sealed residential towers. Automated announcements continue repeating evacuation orders that are several years old.

Pixel-art palette:

* Black concrete
* Wet violet streets
* Toxic green quarantine lamps
* Red emergency strobes
* Pale holographic advertisements

## Official Mission Context

A synthetic terrorist cell has released a neurological pathogen inside Ashfall District.

Zero Division is deployed to restore containment, rescue surviving security personnel, and eliminate the cell’s command structure.

## Narrative Purpose

B01 establishes the crew as competent special-forces operatives and introduces the first three playable characters:

* ROOK
* VECTOR
* RAM

The biome also introduces the first subtle contradictions surrounding the crew.

Possible environmental clues include:

* Residents recognizing members of the crew.
* A memorial wall containing familiar names or symbols.
* Quarantine scanners classifying ROOK as authorized biological property.
* Old security personnel remembering operations that ROOK does not recall.
* References to soldiers being replaced, restored, or printed.
* Data systems recognizing crew profiles that should not exist in civilian infrastructure.

OPERATOR explains these incidents as corrupted records, enemy manipulation, neurological contamination, or false data introduced by the terrorist cell.

The explanations remain believable enough that the crew continues to trust OPERATOR.

## M01 — Cold Deployment

### Playable Character

* ROOK

### Supporting Characters

* OPERATOR communicates over the radio.
* VECTOR is heard over the radio but is not yet playable.

### Mission Premise

ROOK is deployed directly into an active containment operation inside a burning residential tower.

There is no extended introduction.

OPERATOR provides objectives while VECTOR reports from another part of the district. Their dialogue implies that ROOK and VECTOR have completed many operations together.

### Mission Objectives

* Restore power to the residential sector.
* Rescue a trapped security team.
* Eliminate infected drones.
* Reach the district transit connection.

### Gameplay Function

M01 is the immediate gameplay introduction.

The mission teaches:

* Basic movement
* Combat
* Tactical positioning
* Environmental interaction
* ROOK’s primary abilities
* OPERATOR’s mission guidance

### Narrative Beats

A rescued security officer recognizes ROOK.

The officer addresses ROOK by a different rank and claims they served together during an operation approximately eight years earlier.

ROOK does not remember the officer or the operation.

The officer may assume ROOK is joking, suffering from neural damage, or pretending not to remember.

OPERATOR explains that neurological exposure can cause victims to project familiar identities onto soldiers and instructs ROOK to continue the mission.

Additional optional clues may include:

* A scanner briefly reporting ROOK as registered biological equipment.
* An apartment containing a damaged image of a soldier resembling ROOK.
* A civilian beginning to say, “They printed—” before communications or environmental noise interrupts them.
* An old Zero Division symbol appearing beneath newer quarantine markings.

None of these clues should interrupt the mission’s pace or provide a clear answer.

### Narrative Purpose

* Establish ROOK as the initial point-of-view character.
* Introduce OPERATOR as a reliable mission commander.
* Introduce VECTOR through radio dialogue.
* Establish that ROOK and VECTOR already know one another.
* Present the crew as an experienced operational unit.
* Introduce the first identity contradiction.
* Avoid explaining the wider world too early.

### Character State

ROOK begins with the default subclass:

* **TACTICAL**

The name “Tactical” is not initially presented as a subclass name. It appears to be ROOK’s normal combat configuration.

# First HUB Introduction

After M01, the player enters the crew HUB for the first time.

## HUB Function

The HUB introduces:

* ROOK
* VECTOR
* OPERATOR’s mission interface
* Character selection
* Equipment management
* Mission briefings
* Crew dialogue
* Several apparently decorative or inactive structures

The HUB should feel familiar and functional to the characters even though it is new to the player.

ROOK and VECTOR move through the space as if they have used it many times before.

## Hidden Crew Stations

The HUB contains four crew stations from the beginning.

Only two stations are active and clearly readable:

* ROOK’s station
* VECTOR’s station

The other two stations are powered down.

At first, the inactive stations should resemble ordinary HUB decoration, maintenance equipment, storage machinery, sealed wall structures, or technical infrastructure.

They are not labeled.

They do not have interaction prompts.

The camera does not highlight them.

A player who does not examine the environment should not immediately understand that the HUB was built for four characters.

### Shared Visual Language

All four stations share subtle design elements:

* Similar floor mounting
* Matching structural frames
* Equivalent power connections
* A small emblem plate in the same position
* Repeated equipment attachment points
* Similar chamber dimensions
* Equal spacing within the HUB

The inactive stations conceal these similarities through:

* Poor lighting
* Closed panels
* Missing equipment
* Disconnected power cables
* Nearby crates or machinery
* Visual integration with the HUB walls

Careful players may suspect that the inactive structures have a purpose, but they should not receive confirmation.

### RAM’s Hidden Emblem

One inactive station contains RAM’s emblem.

Before RAM appears, the emblem should look like:

* A manufacturer’s mark
* A structural reinforcement symbol
* A hazard icon
* An industrial decoration

Its significance should become clear only after the player sees RAM.

The fourth station also contains a separate emblem belonging to RELAY, but the player has no context for it yet.

## M02 — Containment Doctrine

### Character Selection

Before the mission, the player selects one character in the HUB:

* ROOK
* VECTOR

The selected character remains playable for the entire mission.

There is no free character switching inside the mission.

### Character States

ROOK:

* Tactical

VECTOR:

* Scout

As with ROOK, “Scout” initially appears to be VECTOR’s normal class rather than one of several possible configurations.

### Mission Premise

Zero Division enters a sealed hospital believed to be producing or distributing the neurological pathogen.

The facility is still operating under automated containment procedures.

Medical systems classify patients as infected and direct security machines to destroy them.

### Mission Structure

ROOK and VECTOR approach the hospital differently.

#### ROOK Route

Focus:

* Direct tactical combat
* Defensive positioning
* Controlled entry
* Protecting survivors
* Managing hostile security systems

#### VECTOR Route

Focus:

* Speed
* Scouting
* Precision traversal
* Alternate access points
* Bypassing security systems

The routes may overlap at major objectives and the boss arena, but they should contain character-specific obstacles and discoveries.

The player does not switch characters during the mission.

### Boss

The mission ends with the first major boss encounter.

Possible boss concept:

* A hospital containment commander
* A neurological quarantine machine
* A security unit altered by corrupted Imprint data
* A medical combat platform protecting the restricted archive

### Primary Discovery

The supposed infection is not contagious in the expected biological sense.

The patients’ neurological patterns have been rewritten or destabilized after exposure to leaked Imprint data.

The hospital’s systems treat the altered patients as corrupted assets rather than sick civilians.

### Suspicious Data Clue

The selected character recovers a damaged data fragment from the hospital’s restricted archive.

The fragment may contain:

* A partial manufacturing reference
* An encrypted facility identifier
* An unusual neural signature
* Coordinates hidden inside medical records
* A repeated “IZ” prefix
* A transmission path leading outside Ashfall District

The clue does not yet identify Helix Foundry clearly.

OPERATOR considers the fragment secondary to the containment mission and sends it for later analysis.

RELAY becomes interested in the data and begins examining it independently as part of her technical role.

At this point, the player does not meet RELAY and may not yet know that she is another member of the crew.

### HUB State After M02

There is no new crew reveal after M02.

* ROOK and VECTOR’s stations remain active.
* The two inactive stations remain unchanged.
* No dialogue explains the crew’s total size.
* No interface explicitly reveals additional playable characters.
* The suspicious data fragment becomes the only direct setup for RELAY’s later investigation.

### Gameplay Purpose

* Introduce HUB-based character selection.
* Establish the gameplay differences between ROOK and VECTOR.
* Introduce the first major boss.
* Demonstrate character-specific mission routes.
* Introduce Imprints as a dangerous and poorly understood phenomenon.
* Provide the clue that RELAY later investigates.

### Narrative Purpose

* Strengthen the identity mystery.
* Show that the official pathogen explanation is incomplete.
* Introduce systems that recognize people as data or assets.
* Give the crew a credible reason to investigate another facility.
* Keep OPERATOR trustworthy while showing that OPERATOR does not possess every answer.

## M03 — No Survivors Logged

### Character Selection

Before the mission, the player selects in the HUB:

* ROOK
* VECTOR

The selected character remains playable throughout the mission.

RAM is not available for selection.

### Mission Premise

The recovered hospital records identify a command signal connected to the supposed terrorist cell.

Zero Division assaults the signal’s source inside an abandoned quarantine command center.

The facility appears to have once belonged to Zero Division or an earlier version of the same organization.

### Mission Structure

The selected character advances through the command center using their established route style.

#### ROOK Route

Focus:

* Defensive combat
* Tactical room clearing
* Holding positions
* Coordinating automated defenses

#### VECTOR Route

Focus:

* Rapid infiltration
* Reconnaissance
* Precision platforming
* Disabling security nodes

Environmental clues may include:

* Outdated Zero Division equipment
* Storage bays designed for humanoid units
* Mission logs with missing personnel names
* Records referring to field units by batch or validation number
* Architecture resembling parts of the HUB

### Boss Encounter

The selected character reaches an obsolete Zero Division soldier or command unit.

The target possesses facial architecture, movement patterns, or neural data resembling one of the protagonists.

The boss is protected by armor, shielding, or a structural defense that ROOK and VECTOR cannot break through normal means.

The encounter should communicate that the player currently lacks the correct capability.

It should feel intentionally unwinnable without becoming a prolonged fake battle.

### RAM Intervention

At the critical moment, RAM enters the mission.

RAM breaches the structure surrounding the boss, breaks its defenses, or destroys the mechanism sustaining its protection.

His arrival is fast, destructive, and unexpected.

The selected character then completes the encounter with RAM’s assistance.

RAM is not yet playable during this mission.

### RAM Foreshadowing Payoff

RAM’s armor, equipment, or breaching weapon contains the same emblem that was visible on one of the inactive HUB structures.

For players who did not inspect the HUB:

* RAM appears to be a completely new character.
* His arrival is a surprise.
* There was no obvious indication that another playable crew member existed.

For observant players:

* RAM’s emblem may look familiar.
* His design may resemble the inactive HUB structure.
* They may suspect that the structure was connected to him.

The connection is not fully confirmed until the crew returns to the HUB.

### Boss Transmission

Before being destroyed, the obsolete soldier transmits a damaged message.

Possible final line:

> “You are not the rescue team. You are the replacement.”

The transmission becomes corrupted during the destruction of the command center.

OPERATOR attempts to recover the complete message but is unable to do so.

The scene should not imply that OPERATOR deliberately deletes it.

### Additional Data Connection

The command center contains data using the same signature as the fragment recovered in M02.

The two data sources together point toward an industrial facility outside Ashfall District.

RELAY continues following the signal through remote networks, maintenance systems, and archived logistics routes.

### Narrative Purpose

* Introduce RAM through action rather than exposition.
* Establish RAM as the crew’s breaching and destructive-force specialist.
* Show that some enemies and structures require specific crew abilities.
* Reward observant players who noticed RAM’s inactive station.
* Connect the hospital data to a larger Zero Division mystery.
* Introduce the possibility that the active crew replaced earlier versions of themselves.

# RAM Returns to the HUB

After M03, RAM returns to the HUB with ROOK and VECTOR.

## Station Activation

RAM approaches one of the previously inactive structures.

The station powers on.

Its activation reveals:

* Hidden lighting
* Opening panels
* Equipment mounts
* A character interface
* RAM’s illuminated emblem
* A structural design matching ROOK and VECTOR’s stations

The player should now understand:

> The structure was always RAM’s crew station.

The HUB itself does not need to transform significantly.

The reveal comes from power, lighting, equipment, opened panels, and the player’s new understanding of the space.

## Fourth Station Realization

Once RAM’s station becomes active, the visual pattern of the HUB becomes readable.

The player can now identify:

* ROOK’s station
* VECTOR’s station
* RAM’s station
* One remaining inactive station

The final station is still not labeled, but it is now clearly recognizable as belonging to another crew member.

## Reunion Dialogue

The HUB dialogue establishes that RAM is not a new recruit.

ROOK and VECTOR already know him.

RAM behaves as though he is returning to his normal place after a separate operation.

During the reunion, the crew receives an update concerning RELAY.

The dialogue communicates:

* RELAY is the final member of the crew.
* RELAY was investigating the suspicious data recovered in the sealed hospital during M02.
* The data trail led her to Helix Foundry.
* She entered the facility through a technical or infrastructure route.
* She found additional suspicious data.
* She locked herself inside a restricted section of the facility.
* Communications with her became unstable or stopped.
* The reason she sealed herself inside is not yet known.
* OPERATOR treats the situation as a rescue and data-recovery mission.

A compact version of the information is:

> RELAY followed the data trace recovered from the Ashfall hospital. It led her to Helix Foundry. She found something inside and locked herself in before communications went down.

The crew does not interpret RELAY’s actions as distrust toward OPERATOR.

They assume she encountered an automated security response, a data purge, or another immediate threat inside the facility.

RAM is assigned because he can force a path through the foundry’s industrial lockdown.

## RAM Character State

RAM becomes selectable in the HUB after M03.

RAM begins with:

* **BREACHER**

The Breacher name remains hidden until RAM later unlocks another subclass.

# B02 — Industrial Resurrection Complex

## Working Name

**B02: Helix Foundry**

## Environment

A colossal automated weapons and biotechnology plant.

Robotic arms assemble drones, prosthetics, organs, artificial nervous systems, and humanoid combat bodies along the same production lines.

The upper facility resembles an industrial weapons factory.

The deeper levels resemble a slaughterhouse disguised as a semiconductor plant.

Pixel-art palette:

* Rust red
* Furnace orange
* Surgical white
* Oil-black machinery
* Cold cyan interfaces

## Official Mission Context

Helix Foundry is believed to be producing illegal combat bodies, neurological weapons, and synthetic soldiers for insurgent groups.

RELAY entered the facility while tracing data recovered in Ashfall District and is now trapped inside a restricted sector.

Zero Division must recover RELAY, secure the data, and stop the production systems.

## Narrative Purpose

B02 completes the playable crew roster and strongly suggests that biological soldiers can be manufactured like machines.

The crew discovers evidence that bodies resembling them were produced inside the facility.

The biome introduces:

* RAM as a fully playable character
* RELAY as a physical and playable crew member
* Destructible and character-specific routes
* Imprints as gameplay objects
* The first major shared progression unlock
* The possibility that the crew’s bodies and identities were constructed

## M04 — Production Halt

### Playable Character

* RAM

RAM is selected in the HUB before the mission.

There is no character switching during M04.

### Mission Premise

RELAY followed the data signature recovered from the Ashfall hospital and command center.

The signal led her to Helix Foundry.

Rather than approaching through the main industrial entrances, RELAY entered through a concealed route connected to her role:

* A maintenance data network
* An automated logistics tunnel
* A communications relay
* An underground utility system
* A remote-access transport line

Her route is narrow, technical, and built around system access rather than direct combat.

This explains why she did not encounter ROOK, VECTOR, or RAM earlier.

Inside the foundry, RELAY discovered that the suspicious data was connected to active biological production systems.

She entered a restricted archive to retrieve more information.

When the facility initiated an automated purge or security lockdown, RELAY sealed herself inside the archive sector to prevent the data from being destroyed and to keep the hostile system from reaching her position.

She is protecting the data from the facility itself, not from OPERATOR.

### Mission Structure

RAM enters through the main industrial levels and forces a path toward RELAY.

The mission emphasizes:

* Destructible barriers
* Heavy environmental hazards
* Forced entry
* Structural collapse
* Industrial machinery
* Routes only RAM can open
* Momentum-based combat
* Breaching reinforced production areas

### Primary Objectives

* Disable three production lines.
* Breach the central manufacturing sector.
* Reach RELAY’s last known location.
* Prevent the facility from purging its archive.
* Extract RELAY and the recovered data.

### Environmental Clues

As RAM advances, he encounters increasingly disturbing evidence:

* Empty humanoid shells resembling crew members.
* Unfinished bodies with familiar proportions or facial structures.
* Production labels containing the prefix “IZ.”
* Manufacturing dates more recent than the crew’s remembered childhoods.
* Equipment designed around the crew’s neural profiles.
* Rejected bodies stored as defective industrial products.
* Four recurring biological templates matching the crew.

The similarities should begin ambiguously.

Early bodies may be incomplete enough to dismiss as coincidence.

The deeper production levels make the connection increasingly difficult to ignore.

### RELAY’s Situation

RELAY remains in communication intermittently.

Her dialogue reveals that:

* She entered the facility voluntarily to follow the data trace.
* She expected to find records or an abandoned server.
* She instead discovered active production systems.
* The archive began deleting itself.
* She sealed the sector to preserve the evidence.
* The lockdown then trapped her inside.
* Her technical route cannot support RAM’s size or equipment.
* RAM must reach her through the industrial structure.

### Meeting RELAY

RAM eventually breaches the archive sector and reaches RELAY.

This is the first time the player sees RELAY physically.

The characters treat the reunion as the recovery of an existing crew member, not the introduction of a stranger.

RELAY may be irritated by RAM’s destructive approach but relieved that he arrived.

Their interaction should establish a familiar crew dynamic.

### Final Discovery

The archive contains a system capable of recognizing the crew’s neural profiles.

RELAY cannot fully access it during M04 because it requires direct compatibility from multiple crew members.

She extracts the relevant hardware, data core, or system fragment and returns with RAM to the HUB.

### Narrative Purpose

* Make RAM fully playable.
* Introduce RELAY physically.
* Establish RELAY as an active investigator rather than a passive captive.
* Explain why RELAY followed a separate route from the combat crew.
* Reveal that crew-like bodies are being manufactured.
* Introduce the “IZ” designation.
* Set up the four compatibility trials in M05.

# RELAY Returns to the HUB

After M04, RELAY enters the HUB.

## Final Station Activation

RELAY approaches the last inactive structure.

The station activates and reveals its true function.

Its design may emphasize:

* Network interfaces
* Neural connection equipment
* Data projection
* Signal routing
* Remote-system control
* Imprint analysis tools

The player now understands the complete HUB layout.

The four structures visible from the beginning were crew stations for:

* ROOK
* VECTOR
* RAM
* RELAY

The HUB is now fully active for the first time.

## Crew Assembly

The four crew members are physically reunited in the HUB.

RELAY begins analyzing the data recovered from Helix Foundry.

OPERATOR supports the analysis and authorizes the crew to connect the recovered system to the HUB.

The crew expects to retrieve information about the foundry and the altered civilians from Ashfall District.

Instead, the system recognizes all four crew members directly.

## M05 — The Four Trials

### Primary Playable Character

* RELAY

### Secondary Playable Characters

During the trials, control switches between RELAY and the other crew members:

* RELAY and ROOK
* RELAY and VECTOR
* RELAY and RAM
* RELAY alone, followed by interaction with all three crew profiles

Character switching is scripted.

The player cannot freely select or change characters during the mission.

### Mission Premise

RELAY attempts to extract, reconstruct, and decrypt the data recovered from Helix Foundry.

The recovered system refuses ordinary access.

It identifies the crew’s neural signatures and initiates four adaptive compatibility trials.

The trials initially resemble security simulations designed to protect classified information.

As the mission continues, it becomes clear that the system is not merely verifying identity.

It is testing compatibility between the crew and stored Imprints.

### System Identification

During the activation sequence, the system identifies the protagonists as:

> IMPRINT ZERO — ACTIVE FIELD VALIDATION UNITS

OPERATOR suggests that the designation may refer to their equipment, combat systems, or mission hardware.

The explanation is possible but incomplete.

The crew remains focused on completing the trials and accessing the data.

* RELAY
* ROOK

* Tactical combat
* Positioning
* Command decisions

ROOK navigates a reconstructed combat scenario while RELAY manipulates doors, defenses, targeting systems, and environmental controls.

### Playable Characters

Scripted control alternates between:

* RELAY
* RAM

### Focus

* Destruction
* Momentum
* Environmental interaction
* Opening paths for RELAY
* Breaking corrupted system structures

### Trial Structure

RAM moves through a simulation of the Helix production system while RELAY identifies unstable nodes and directs him toward structural weaknesses.

## Trial 4 — RELAY

### Playable Character

* RELAY

The other crew members appear through linked profiles, remote actions, or controlled system echoes.

### Focus

* Network control
* Data-space traversal
* System corruption
* Imprint extraction
* Managing all four crew profiles

### Trial Structure

RELAY enters the deepest layer of the recovered system.

She must prevent the data from collapsing while aligning the four crew signatures.

The final sequence reveals that the trials were not created as general security tests.

They were designed specifically for the four crew profiles.

### Narrative Clues

* The system recognizes RELAY as both a field unit and an access mechanism.
* The four crew profiles form a complete operational set.
* Their identities exist inside systems external to their bodies.
* The system expects the crew to receive stored Imprints.
* References imply that previous field validation units may have existed.

RELAY extracts the Imprints before the system shuts down or corrupts itself.

The crew does not yet recover enough information to understand the full truth.

# Act I Mystery Progression

## M01

Someone remembers ROOK from an operation he does not remember.

## M02

The supposed pathogen is revealed to be neurological corruption caused by leaked Imprint data.

RELAY begins investigating a suspicious data trace recovered from the hospital.

## M03

An obsolete Zero Division soldier resembles the active crew and calls them replacements.

RAM appears unexpectedly and returns to his previously hidden HUB station.

The remaining inactive station is revealed indirectly as belonging to another crew member.

## M04

RELAY is identified as the fourth crew member.

RAM rescues her from Helix Foundry.

The crew discovers recently manufactured bodies resembling themselves.

## M05

The Helix system recognizes all four crew members as Imprint Zero field validation units.

The crew receives Imprints through trials designed around their specific neural profiles.

Act I ends without explaining whether the protagonists are clones, replacements, reconstructed identities, manufactured soldiers, or something else.
