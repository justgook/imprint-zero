## Shared sensory cue

> **Accepted** — All four memories contain a fragmented evacuation announcement: the same voice and clipped phrase, **“Remain at your assigned station.”** Each memory presents it differently.

The announcement suggests institutional continuity without proving that the events occurred together. It confirms neither a shared location nor chronology.
