## Purpose

M05 — The Four Trials recovers four Imprints, one matched to each crew member's default Specialization. Completing all four trials unlocks Overdrive globally; they are one campaign unlock rather than four separate progression tracks.

## Known Imprints

### ROOK — Tactical

Awakens Overdrive for ROOK's control, positioning, and planned-combat Specialization.

### VECTOR — Ghost

Awakens [[Characters/Vector#c21|Blind Spot]] for VECTOR's stealth, Ambush, and surface-traversal Specialization.

### RAM — Impact

Awakens [[Characters/Ram#c31|Counteroffensive]] for RAM's shield defence, Counterforce, and advancing Breach Specialization.

### RELAY — Wire

Awakens [[Characters/Relay#c41--wire|Total Integration]] for RELAY's machine-integration Specialization.

The exact transformations and memory revelations for all four Imprints remain to be defined.
