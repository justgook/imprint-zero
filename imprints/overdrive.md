## Purpose

M05 — The Four Trials recovers four Imprints, one matched to each crew member’s default subclass. Completing all four trials unlocks Overdrive globally; they are one campaign unlock rather than four separate progression tracks.

## Known Imprints

### ROOK — Tactical

Awakens Overdrive for ROOK’s control, positioning, and planned-combat subclass.

### VECTOR — Ghost

Awakens [[Characters/Vector#c21|Blind Spot]] for VECTOR's stealth, Ambush, and surface-traversal Specialization.

### RAM — Breacher

Awakens Overdrive for RAM’s defense-breaking and route-opening subclass.

### RELAY — Wire

Awakens Overdrive for RELAY’s direct device interaction and local-control subclass.

The exact transformations and memory revelations for all four Imprints remain to be defined.
