## Narrative role

> **Accepted** — The **Old Man** is the experiment network's last registered predecessor-city civilian and a literal survivor of CIVITAS-00.

He gives the crew two physical leads during [[Missions/HS01|HS01 — The Last Civilian]] without authoritatively explaining the experiment network, OPERATOR, or the relationship between the destination cities.

## Survival and continuity

The Old Man has survived through repaired organs, replacement parts, and partial memory restoration. His biology and records establish survival across the predecessor experiment, but they do not prove uninterrupted bodily, mnemonic, or personal continuity.

“Last civilian” is the experiment network's registration category. It does not mean he is the only living person in Republic of Dust. Current residents descend from abandoned experimental subjects whom the network no longer recognizes as registered predecessor-city civilians.

## Campaign function

| Function | Accepted direction |

| Commitment boundary | Allow both immediate staging areas to be inspected; boarding either shielded transport commits the campaign. |
| Evidence boundary | Offer useful testimony without becoming an unquestionable historian, leader, or narrator of objective truth. |

The interface presents destinations and known evidence rather than Mind/Body labels, ending types, route capabilities, or moral judgments. The Old Man does not explain the destinations as a game route choice.

## Relationship to Republic of Dust

The Old Man lives among descendants who manually rebuilt CIVITAS-00 while hiding from the experiment network. His network registration gives him unusual historical significance but does not automatically grant political authority over Republic of Dust.

Residents may preserve, challenge, reinterpret, or know more recent local history than he does. Later population work must not reduce the city to one elder's exposition.

## RELAY interaction boundary

> **Accepted** — The Old Man deliberately destroyed his communication interface, leaving no connection for RELAY to hack.

His repaired cybernetics do not provide a substitute path into his thoughts or memories. Information must come through ordinary dialogue, surviving records, and physical evidence; RELAY's inability to connect does not make his testimony objectively reliable. [[Missions/HS01#relay-deployment-variation|HS01]] owns RELAY's refusal and the resulting crew conversation.

## Knowledge boundary

The Old Man may know:

- that Republic of Dust preceded the current successor city;
- practical wired and shielded routes used to avoid network detection;
- remembered or recorded fragments concerning Velvet Exchange and Cathedral of Deliverance;
- how to activate or provide the two physical leads.

He must not objectively establish:

- OPERATOR's identity or authority;
- Controller's relationship to OPERATOR;
- who originated or owns the experiment network;
- the network's full geographic scale;
- complete histories of Velvet Exchange or Cathedral of Deliverance;
- that his restored memories are complete, unedited, or originally his;
- whether his present self is continuous with the person first registered by CIVITAS-00.
