An **NPC** is a non-playable campaign actor whose authored identity or agency extends beyond a tactical boss Encounter. Speaking during a fight does not make a Boss an NPC. Dual classification is reserved for entities with substantial independent campaign agency beyond the fight; Controller is the accepted exception.

## Accepted NPCs

- [[NPCs/Operator|OPERATOR]] — the crew's familiar mission-coordination voice and a participant in CIVITAS experiment traffic; its identity, embodiment, authorship, and relationship to Controller remain intentionally unresolved.
- [[NPCs/Cradle Security System|Cradle Security System]] — the distinct facility authority controlling utilization and production security while OPERATOR is absent.
- [[NPCs/Controller|Controller]] — the proven local orchestration authority for CIVITAS-01 whose complete embodiment and relationship to OPERATOR remain unresolved.
- [[NPCs/Old Man|Old Man]] — CIVITAS-00's last registered civilian and a fallible surviving witness with uncertain bodily and mnemonic continuity.
- [[NPCs/Continuum|Continuum]] — a collective mind preserving distinguishable joined identities without one permanent controlling voice.

## NPC-page rule

Each accepted NPC page records:

- the name or designation perceived by the crew;
- campaign function and first appearance;
- presentation, voice, and communication channel;
- observed actions and demonstrated knowledge;
- relationship with the crew;
- claims made by the NPC versus facts established independently;
- unresolved identity, motive, authority, and allegiance;
- required dialogue, visual, and gameplay evidence.

Entities whose primary authored role is a boss Encounter belong in the [[Bosses/Overview|Boss Catalogue]], including when they speak or make narrative claims.

Do not convert uncertainty into false biography. If the crew experiences one consistent entity while its actual nature remains unknown, document both facts separately.
