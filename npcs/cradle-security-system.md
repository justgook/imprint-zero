## Narrative role

> **Accepted** — The **Cradle Security System** is the facility controller encountered across the Empty Barracks and Cradle while OPERATOR is unreachable.

It classifies returned crews, enforces utilization, protects production, and responds to the current crew's intact arrival as an unhandled condition. Its distinct voice, vocabulary, and interfaces prevent the campaign from treating every institutional system as OPERATOR.

## Presentation boundary

The Cradle Security System uses a voice, vocabulary, and interface treatment distinct from OPERATOR. It frames people as batches, material, utilization targets, production inputs, and security exceptions rather than as a familiar deployed team.

Do not gradually blend its presentation into OPERATOR. The later transmission shift toward Controller occurs in Ashfall Spire, not during the Cradle sequence.

## Identity boundary

The following remain unresolved:

- whether it is one intelligence, several facility processes, or a security persona;
- whether it possesses reflective agency beyond its directives;
- who authored or last changed those directives;
- whether OPERATOR can command it directly or only possesses accepted credentials;
- whether it reports to Controller, shares infrastructure with Controller, or is operationally separate;
- whether any part persists after the Cradle is destroyed.

The crew may confidently distinguish its presentation from OPERATOR without knowing what either entity ultimately is.
