## Narrative role

> **Accepted** — **OPERATOR** is the institutional designation for the recognizable voice or persona that coordinates the crew's deployments. The consistency of that presentation is observable; the continuity and nature of the entity behind it are intentionally unresolved.

OPERATOR is neither a confirmed individual nor a generic synonym for the experiment network. The campaign establishes increasing institutional involvement while preserving uncertainty about embodiment, authorship, authority, and allegiance.

## What the crew perceives

The crew experiences OPERATOR as familiar operational support that:

- delivers and clarifies Mission objectives;
- frames deployments around genuine immediate public benefits;
- acknowledges completion and extraction;
- interprets institutional records;
- communicates access and authorization;
- responds to operational events through a consistent mission-control voice.

“OPERATOR” describes this observed relationship. It does not prove whether the voice originates orders, interprets them, or relays another authority.

## Relationship to other authorities

| Authority | Accepted relationship boundary |
|---|---|
| **Cradle Security System** | Operates the Empty Barracks and Cradle while OPERATOR is absent. Its distinct presentation must remain separate from OPERATOR. |
| **Controller** | Coordinates the current successor-city experiment and connected facilities. OPERATOR-authenticated traffic crosses Controller infrastructure, but their identities and hierarchy remain unresolved. |
| **Experiment Network** | Connects CIVITAS experiments, Velvet Exchange, Cathedral of Deliverance, and related infrastructure. OPERATOR participates in its traffic without being established as its owner, originator, or supreme authority. |

Evidence may establish access without knowledge, knowledge without authorship, or authorship without ultimate authority. These categories must not be collapsed into one claim.

## Identity boundary

The following remain intentionally unresolved:

- whether OPERATOR is human, artificial, composite, or something else;
- whether one underlying entity produces every appearance;
- whether the designation is a person, role, persona, interface, or relay;
- whether OPERATOR authors orders or communicates orders from elsewhere;
- whether OPERATOR remembers previous deployments outside the current channel context;
- whether OPERATOR and Controller are separate entities, different interfaces, or overlapping functions;
- whether OPERATOR serves, administers, or is itself subject to the experiment network;
- physical location, embodiment, gender, age, and biography.

This ambiguity is the accepted answer, not missing exposition to be resolved by a later identity reveal.

## Presentation and voice

> **Accepted** — OPERATOR is introduced as a voice during active play, not through a body reveal, biography, portrait, or opening cinematic.

The voice is:

- calm and concise;
- operational rather than theatrical;
- reassuring enough that initial obedience feels normal;
- capable of dismissing contradictions without sounding openly malicious;
- recognizably consistent even when underlying continuity is uncertain;
- unmarked by a confirmed gender, age, embodiment, or physical location.

The late-20th-century mission-control tone should create familiarity without directly imitating a specific existing character.
