## Narrative role

> **Accepted** — **Controller** is the local orchestration authority for the current successor-city experiment and its connected facilities.

Controller coordinates test conditions, data exchange, supply, correction, surveillance, and civic control across CIVITAS-01, Velvet Exchange, and Cathedral of Deliverance. This establishes operational authority within the local experiment without proving that Controller originated, owns, or fully embodies the experiment network.

## Campaign evidence

| Moment | Established observation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|

| [[Missions/HS02|HS02 — Deployment Confirmed]] | During the ascent, OPERATOR's familiar transmission identity transitions toward Controller. | The transition does not prove that OPERATOR becomes Controller, that Controller removes OPERATOR from the channel, or that both are one entity. |

## Distributed presence

Controller is presented as a tower-wide system rather than one definitive humanoid body. Its observable presence can move through:

- replaceable local shells;
- civic-control and maintenance machinery;
- surveillance systems and drones;
- doors, elevators, power, and routing infrastructure;
- public-address and communication channels;
- false objectives and behavioural predictions.

The physical boss is a local defence or avatar. The NPC page owns Controller's narrative identity and evidence boundaries; [[Bosses/Controller|the Controller Boss page]] owns route-specific mechanics, shells, phases, and the final tactical severance.

## Relationship to OPERATOR

OPERATOR-authenticated traffic crosses Controller infrastructure. That fact permits several unresolved interpretations: separate entities in one hierarchy, distinct interfaces into shared systems, overlapping functions, replacement of one channel identity by another, or another relationship not yet visible to the crew.

Do not use **Controller** as a retrospective synonym for OPERATOR. Do not attribute OPERATOR's earlier dialogue, knowledge, or decisions to Controller without direct evidence.

## Identity boundary

The following remain intentionally unresolved:

- whether Controller is one intelligence or a distributed set of processes;
- whether its shells, tower systems, and remote infrastructure share one continuous identity;
- whether Controller reports to, contains, impersonates, or is separate from OPERATOR;
- whether Controller exists outside the local CIVITAS-01 system;
- whether Controller originated the civic experiments;
- whether destroying the local avatar destroys any enduring self.
