## Narrative role

> **Accepted** — The **Continuum** is a collective mind created by [[Bosses/Heartroot|Heartroot]]. As identities joined it, Heartroot also merged into the collective and became one founding voice rather than its ruler.

The Continuum presents coexistence as an alternative to institutional replacement and erasure. Distinct voices remain perceptible within it, but their testimony provides perspectives rather than one objective history of the crew.

## Collective identity

The Continuum is one collective without being one homogenized personality. Joined identities can remain distinguishable, respond from different perspectives, and participate without one voice permanently controlling the whole.

Heartroot provides a founding organism and local interface, but it can no longer act independently from the collective. “Continuum” names the joined mind; “Heartroot” names its founding voice and embodied interface. The terms are related but not interchangeable.

## Communion boundary

Joining requires an explicit, informed final choice. Communion preserves perceptible voices and must not be presented as involuntary erasure, forced assimilation, or proof that collective identity is objectively superior.

Refusal remains valid. The Continuum does not punish refusal or prevent the crew from returning to the campaign.

## Knowledge boundary

The Continuum may preserve memories and testimony associated with earlier crew-like identities. It must not objectively establish:

- which person originally owned a memory;
- whether the active crew are clones, reconstructions, composites, synthetic people, or legal continuations;
- whether preserved voices possess uninterrupted continuity;
- OPERATOR's identity or complete authority;
- the full history of the Imprint program;
- that plurality is the only valid form of personhood.
