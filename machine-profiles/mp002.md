## Network — Controlled Unit

| Order | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Follow | Keep pace near RELAY and intercept close attackers. |
| Focus | Commit to a charge into the marked target. |
| Hold | Guard a short lane and charge the first valid enemy crossing it. |
| Direct Control | Run and jump quickly or commit to a ram; cannot wall-move or activate RELAY route locks. |

Line Runner converts horizontal space into pressure. Turning, stopping, and recovery during a committed ram remain constraints rather than free steering.
