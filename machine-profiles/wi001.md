## Behaviour

> **Accepted** — Arc Cutter replaces Wire's Interface Projector primary fire with a short-range continuous cutting beam on the shared eight-direction lattice.

Firing slows RELAY and rapidly builds Access on compatible System Targets. Its short reach and movement penalty preserve exposure while its setup rate exceeds the fallback projector.

## Constraints

- Grants no route access beyond shared Hack.
- Does not remain available alongside another Weapon integration.
- Is not separately destructible outside a full Chassis.
- Exact reach, damage, movement penalty, and Access rate require prototypes.
