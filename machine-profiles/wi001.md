## Behaviour

> **Accepted** — Arc Cutter transforms Wire's [[Equipment/EQ020|Adaptive Chain Sickles]] into a short-range continuous cutting beam driven by RELAY's abstract [[Gameplay/Actions#directional-attack|Directional Attack]] action.

Firing slows RELAY and rapidly builds Access on compatible System Targets. Its short reach and movement penalty preserve exposure while its setup rate exceeds the default sickle form.

## Constraints

- Grants no route access beyond shared Hack.
- Does not remain available alongside another Weapon integration.
- Inherits base power and explicitly compatible passive traits from the equipped Wire adaptive weapon; it does not inherit that item's unintegrated moveset.
- Is not separately destructible outside a full Chassis.
- Exact reach, damage, movement penalty, and Access rate require prototypes.
