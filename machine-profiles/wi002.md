## Behaviour

> **Accepted** — Runner Legs increase Wire's ground speed and acceleration, extend horizontal jump distance, and provide a committed ground burst.

The burst supports approach and escape but cannot turn or stop freely once committed.

## Constraints

- Grants no Wall Run, universal hazard immunity, or unique route access.
- Replaces rather than stacks with another Mobility integration.
- Is not separately destructible outside a full Chassis.
- Exact speed, jump distance, burst duration, and recovery require prototypes.
