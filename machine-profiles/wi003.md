## Behaviour

> **Accepted** — Sensor Array upgrades RELAY's universal coarse Access feedback into exact tactical information.

It displays the exact Access value, moves currently available, delayed-decay countdown, compatibility and resistance tags, authored weak points, and predicted Blackout chain targets. It improves information and planning rather than directly increasing damage.

## Constraints

- Does not discover hidden routes or establish objective narrative truth.
- Replaces rather than stacks with another Systems integration.
- Is not separately destructible outside a full Chassis.
- Exact scan range, weak-point presentation, and information density require prototypes.
