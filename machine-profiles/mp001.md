## Network — Controlled Unit

| Order | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Follow | Hover near RELAY and cut nearby threats within short reach. |
| Focus | Latch onto the marked machine and rapidly build its hack window. |
| Hold | Anchor and cut enemies entering its short control radius. |
| Direct Control | Hover precisely and aim the short-range cutter; cannot activate RELAY route locks. |

Fabricator is a low-damage setup unit. Its value comes from creating access for takeover, integration, or resistant-target hacking rather than replacing a primary attacker.
