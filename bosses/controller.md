## Boss identity

> **Accepted** — The Controller Encounter fights a local avatar and tower-wide defence system, not one definitive humanoid body or proof of Controller's complete embodiment.

The [[NPCs/Controller|Controller NPC page]] owns its campaign identity, institutional role, OPERATOR relationship, and evidence boundaries. This page owns the tactical confrontation in [[Missions/HS03|HS03 — No Original]].

## Encounter system

Controller transfers among replaceable shells and Ashfall Spire infrastructure. The fight may involve:

- civic-control and maintenance machinery;
- surveillance systems and drones;
- doors, elevators, power, and routing infrastructure;
- public-address and communication channels;
- false objectives and behavioural predictions;
- local shells that can be replaced until the route-specific system is disrupted.

The arena and infrastructure participate throughout the Encounter. Defeating one shell before isolating it must not imply that Controller has been wholly destroyed.

## Route-specific advantage

| Route | Crew capability | Tactical function |
|---|---|---|
| HA — Mind | Mnemonic authorization | Identify false memories, corrupt behavioural predictions, and force inconsistent Controller decisions. |
| HB — Body and Control | Service-root and ownership credentials | Disable shell transfer, reroute power, and isolate Controller's physical infrastructure. |

Neither route is superior. Each must support a complete Encounter and the same fundamental ending action.

## Accepted outcome

Either route traps one local avatar long enough for the crew to sever CIVITAS-01 from the experiment network. Destroying that avatar does not prove Controller lacks copies, infrastructure, or continuity elsewhere.

There is no final moral-choice menu after the Encounter. Route differences change mechanics, evidence, dialogue, and interpretation rather than the local outcome.

## Encounter constraints

- Every Character and Specialization must be able to complete the Encounter.
- HA and HB capabilities supplement rather than replace the selected Character's combat identity.
- Replaceable shells and environmental control must read as one distributed Encounter rather than unrelated phases.
- The final vulnerable state must follow from the selected route's established authorization.
- Victory presentation must distinguish severing CIVITAS-01 from destroying the entire experiment network.
