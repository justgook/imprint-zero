## Boss identity

> **Accepted** — **Heartroot** created the process and biological substrate from which the Continuum formed. As other identities joined, Heartroot merged into the resulting collective and became one founding voice within it.

Heartroot is not the Continuum's ruler or sole personality. It remains the collective's central local organism and embodied interface but can no longer act independently from the joined mind it created.

## Campaign function

During [[Missions/MC03|MC03 — Heartroot]], the crew reaches the Continuum's central organism and chooses a local response:

| Response | Accepted meaning |
|---|---|
| Destroy | Reject or disable the encountered local organism. |
| Extract | Remove or contain the encountered local organism for analysis. |
| Accept its mark | Permit a limited local connection or acknowledgement. |

These responses express competing reactions to collective identity. None is declared philosophically correct, and none unlocks or blocks Communion. The hidden survivor flag revealed during MS03 remains the only route condition.

## Relationship to the Continuum

| Term | Accepted distinction |
|---|---|
| **Heartroot** | Founding organism, founding voice, and local embodied interface. |
| **Continuum** | The collective mind formed through Heartroot and the distinguishable identities that joined it. |

Heartroot may speak recognizably through the collective, but its speech is participation rather than unilateral command. Damage, extraction, or alteration of its local form does not establish that the distributed Continuum or every joined identity has been destroyed.

## Identity boundary

The following remain unresolved:

- what Heartroot was before it created the Continuum;
- whether it intended the collective's eventual form;
- how much of its pre-Continuum identity remains distinguishable;
- whether its local organism is unique, replaceable, or one of several interfaces;
- how joined voices influence a response expressed through Heartroot;
- what persists after each MC03 local outcome.

Heartroot's creation of the Continuum does not make its account of identity or crew history objectively correct.

## Encounter ownership

Heartroot's boss page owns its tactical role, communication during the Encounter, local outcomes, and presentation. The [[NPCs/Continuum|Continuum]] page owns the collective mind's broader identity, testimony, and Communion boundaries.
