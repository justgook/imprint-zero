## Visual direction

![Curator body study showing a low spider-like processing body, support and cartridge-reader limbs, multi-bank cabling, rear docking ports and a physical media gripper](content/images/bosses/bs004-concept.png)

> **Working version — Committed body language:** A low, broad central processing/reading body, four robust support assemblies and four finer reader/manipulator assemblies. Multiple rear/side ports connect it to physical storage banks; precise grippers handle cartridges and spools while the support feet anchor to service rails/racks. A compact opaque core and recessed read-heads replace a humanoid face. This is the working visual baseline, not a final sprite or measured hitbox.

The generated sheet shows two views and two hardware details of **one body**, not multiple bosses or phases. Limb topology, scale, articulation and cartridge poses need production review. Protruding readers/transfer assemblies are not approved guns or attacks. Handling a worn cartridge close to its readers while other limbs remain busy suggests attention/appetite without displaying identifiable memories.

The physical wire-web is the archive's connection architecture: insulated radial runs, finer cross-wiring, mechanical anchors and patch interfaces. It is not organic silk, a magical attack field or a holographic graph. The Curator's support/reader/docking anatomy provides family resemblance for task-specialized helpers; resemblance does not prove a biological species, literal reproduction or a creation process.

## Author-only motivation and reveal boundary

> **Working version — Data appetite:** The Curator is a local task-optimized archive authority within the broader experiment infrastructure, not its proven owner. Its intended work selects and arranges useful mnemonic/emotional material; its failure is an appetite for retaining and acquiring ever more interesting experience. Contradictory memories, unusual attachments and emotional variation become material it cannot treat as expendable. Accumulation and repeated processing consume capacity, undermine orderly output and drive harmful over-collection. This is objective drift, not a settled conscious rebellion or proof of an inner human-like self.

The crew receives ordinary containment/anti-extraction Missions with genuine immediate benefits. Behind that framing, correction of this archive's deviation also serves the wider system. OPERATOR-associated deployment does not establish whether the familiar voice originates the order, relays it, owns the experiment or understands every consequence. Do not put the experiment's full purpose, iterative cities or this hidden correction rationale into Act II briefing dialogue.

The appetite should be inferred through retention/handling, crowded connections and unwillingness to relinquish media, not a confession that explains the whole system. Its identity claims remain interested testimony even when the physical apparatus confirms real exploitation.
