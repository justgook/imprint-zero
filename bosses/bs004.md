### Face and borrowed speech

An actual biological woman's head hangs at the bottom, with intact human facial tissue and hair responding to gravity. A small unstable projected human visage sits a short distance **in front of the physical face**; the real head remains visible behind it. The projection is a separate replay layer, not a transparent head, helmet/mask substitute, giant archive hologram or identifiable donor/RELAY portrait. The head/brain is manufactured functional substrate; its existence does not settle personhood, former identity or which stored fragments it experiences. Expressions and speech can change as stored fragments replay: one remembered phrase may begin in one voice and finish in another, or an intimate fragment may become an instruction to machinery. Keep a consistent operational intent beneath the discontinuity; this is not arbitrary word salad or an authoritative account of where the fragments originated.

Physical cables still form the maintained wire-web; allowing a local face projection does not turn the connections into holographic graphs, organic silk or magic. The Curator's grip/reader/docking language informs task-specialized helpers without proving biological lineage or literal reproduction.

## Author-only motivation and reveal boundary

> **Working version — Data appetite:** The Curator is a local task-optimized archive authority within the broader experiment infrastructure, not its proven owner. Its intended work selects and arranges useful mnemonic/emotional material; its failure is an appetite for retaining and acquiring ever more interesting experience. Contradictory memories, unusual attachments and emotional variation become material it cannot treat as expendable. Accumulation and repeated processing consume capacity, undermine orderly output and drive harmful over-collection. This is objective drift, not a settled conscious rebellion or proof of an inner human-like self.

The crew receives ordinary containment/anti-extraction Missions with genuine immediate benefits. Behind that framing, correction of this archive's deviation also serves the wider system. OPERATOR-associated deployment does not establish whether the familiar voice originates the order, relays it, owns the experiment or understands every consequence. Do not put the experiment's full purpose, iterative cities or this hidden correction rationale into Act II briefing dialogue.

The appetite should be inferred through retention/handling, crowded connections and unwillingness to relinquish media, not a confession that explains the whole system. Its identity claims remain interested testimony even when the physical apparatus confirms real exploitation. Resemblance does not prove a biological species or explain what, if anything, experiences the borrowed speech.

## Encounter and outcome handoff

> **Working version — Draw the keeper down:** The Curator rapidly traverses walls/storage frames, using its limbs concurrently. The player damages an authored, reachable vault handling/connection assembly; the Curator descends to repair/recover its collection, adopting a grounded posture that exposes a reachable processing interface. Ordinary attacks exploit that opening before it withdraws. The thematic decision is threatening its work to draw out its keeper, not pursuing it onto the ceiling.

The first working target is vault infrastructure rather than compulsory erasure of victim memories. Exact destruction, repair and evidence-preservation semantics remain to be authored; do not turn every storage bay or wire into a destructible bait target. All Characters and unlocked Specializations need ordinary-kit access to the bait and vulnerability window. Wall movement is the Boss's capability, not a required player route verb.

Reading/alignment lasers and launched empty media crates are working attack directions derived from archive tasks. Rapid repositioning must lead to readable, committed attacks with safe margins for slow RAM and short-range RELAY. No numerical damage, laser coverage, crate physics, timing or phase quota is allocated by the concept.
