A **Boss** is an entity whose primary authored role is a boss Encounter. Boss pages own tactical decisions, Encounter structure, communication during the fight, local outcomes, and presentation.

## Catalogue boundary

Speaking, expressing a philosophy, or making narrative claims does not also make a Boss an NPC. The [[NPCs/Overview|NPC Catalogue]] owns actors with substantial campaign identity or agency beyond a boss fight. Dual classification is exceptional; Controller qualifies because it orchestrates CIVITAS-01 and Ashfall Spire beyond its final avatar Encounter.

## Accepted Bosses

- [[Bosses/Heartroot|Heartroot]] — the Continuum's founding organism, merged founding voice, local interface, and MC03 Guardian.
- [[Bosses/Controller|Controller — Local Avatar]] — the distributed Ashfall Spire defence and final Act IV Encounter; Controller also has an NPC page because its campaign agency extends beyond the fight.

Additional accepted Mission bosses receive pages during the later boss population pass.

## Boss-page rule

Each accepted Boss page records:

- the tactical decision and Encounter function;
- phases, states, attacks, and vulnerabilities;
- communication and narrative claims made during the Encounter;
- local outcomes and campaign consequences;
- established evidence versus interested or uncertain claims;
- visual, audio, arena, and production requirements.
