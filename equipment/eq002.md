## Role

> **Accepted** — The Kunai supports stealth openings at range without leaving Ghost unable to attack nearby enemies.

[[Characters/Vector#c21|Ghost VECTOR]] owns default-loadout references and the Ambush Critical modifier. This page owns the Kunai's intrinsic attacks.

## Input

| Condition | Attack |
|---|---|
| Valid enemy within melee range | Perform a quick Kunai melee strike |
| No valid enemy within melee range | Throw a Kunai in the aimed direction |

The proximity switch follows ROOK's contextual-melee pattern: it occurs only when the player attacks and never initiates automatically.

## Attack behaviour

| Attack | Intended profile |
|---|---|
| Melee strike | Fast, close-range attack with short recovery |
| Throw | Silent straight-line projectile with short-to-medium effective range |

> **Accepted** — Kunai throws use an unlimited abstracted supply with no reload.

> **Accepted** — Ghost may use both Kunai attack forms during Wall Run and while attached with Climbing Claws.
