## Role

> **Accepted** — Flash Charges interrupt enemy awareness and response long enough for Phase VECTOR to begin a direct approach.

They support aggression rather than concealment: affected enemies know they are under attack, but briefly lose effective perception and targeting.

## Effect

| Target state | Result |
|---|---|
| Valid standard enemy in effect radius and line of sight | Briefly disoriented and unable to track or respond normally |
| Protected enemy or Boss | Uses authored resistance or reduced effect rather than guaranteed full disable |
| Phase VECTOR in ordinary state | Disoriented by the same valid effect |
| Phase VECTOR during Phase Shift | Unaffected while occupying the adjacent phase layer |

> **Accepted** — Flash Charges deal no direct damage and use a limited carried supply. The intended aggressive sequence is throw, Phase Shift through the detonation, and attack disoriented enemies; poor timing may interrupt Phase's own approach.

## Specialization boundary

Flash Charges create an opening; they do not grant invisibility, invulnerability, or a separate route capability. [[Characters/Vector#c22|Phase VECTOR]] remains exposed unless she spends a Phase Charge or avoids the response through movement.
