## Role

> **Accepted** — The Armored Gauntlet provides one committed close-range punch rather than a combo.

## Attack behaviour

| Condition | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Idle or movement | Performs the punch and enters normal recovery |
| Planted Guard | Drops shield protection and exits Planted Guard before punching |
| End of Shield Charge | A timed input replaces charge recovery with a follow-up punch that gains stagger and knockback but no automatic damage multiplier |

Gauntlet attacks consume no ammunition or Stability and cannot independently break RAM-reactive barriers.

[[Characters/Ram#c31|Counterforce]] may empower the next Gauntlet hit through an explicit Impact modifier.
