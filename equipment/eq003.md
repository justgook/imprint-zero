## Role

> **Accepted** — The Active Camouflage Module creates stealth openings by breaking enemy detection and target tracking. It never prevents damage.

[[Characters/Vector#c21|Ghost VECTOR]] owns its loadout relationship and Ambush interactions. This page owns concealment activation, charge, and interruption rules.

## Concealment rules

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Activated in open space | Camouflage charge drains continuously |
| Remaining in shadow | Charge drain is substantially reduced or paused |
| Attacking | Concealment ends |
| Taking damage | Concealment ends |
| Direct enemy contact | Concealment ends |
| Successful concealment | Enemy [[Gameplay/Mechanics#enemy-awareness|Tracking]] breaks into Searching |

> **Accepted** — Concealment does not grant invulnerability, collision immunity, or phase traversal.

## Resources

> **Accepted** — The module uses a rechargeable internal charge rather than a carried consumable.

| Recharge condition | Rule |
|---|---|
| Camouflage active | No recharge |
| Camouflage ended | Passive recharge begins after a short delay |
| Ghost takes damage | Passive-recharge delay restarts |
| Ghost remains in shadow | Passive recharge accelerates |
| Ambush Critical hits | Restores a fixed amount immediately, including against a Boss |
