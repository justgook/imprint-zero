## Role

> **Accepted** — Commitment Servos prevent minor stagger from interrupting Onslaught's heavy-attack windup. They do not reduce incoming damage.

## State interaction

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Ordinary movement | No additional protection |
| Heavy-attack windup | Minor stagger cannot interrupt the attack |
| Heavy authored hit | May still interrupt or defeat RAM according to target rules |
| Attack recovery | No additional protection |

The Servos provide no invulnerability, passive mitigation, or resistance outside the authored windup.

> **Accepted** — Commitment Servos consume no charge, ammunition, or carried resource.
