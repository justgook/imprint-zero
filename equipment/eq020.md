## Role

> **Accepted** — Adaptive Chain Sickles are Wire's starter primary weapon and the stable substrate through which Weapon Integrations transform her attacks.

Their default form uses two chained sickles for fast alternating directional strikes, paired cross-slashes, circular sweeps, and committed throws at medium reach. Valid hits build Access on compatible System Targets. They provide RELAY's most flexible base combo structure. The weapon itself is indestructible; this prevents Field Integration from leaving Wire without a valid primary.

## Integration behaviour

A compatible Weapon Integration transforms the adaptive weapon rather than adding a second primary. The equipped `EQ###` substrate continues to supply its base power and compatible passive traits; the installed `WI###` integration owns the transformed moveset, reach, cadence, special behaviour, and presentation. Removing or losing that integration returns the equipped item to its unintegrated form. Later Wire primary variants follow the same substrate rule.

A full Chassis replaces the entire normal attack set until ejection or destruction. Chain attacks cannot grapple terrain, move RELAY through the environment, or create route access.

## Action mapping

The weapon responds to RELAY's abstract [[Gameplay/Actions#directional-attack|Directional Attack]] action. Physical bindings and exact directional resolution remain deferred.

## Resources

> **Accepted** — The weapon has no ammunition or durability resource.

Exact attack cadence, chain reach, recovery, hit regions, damage, and Access buildup require prototypes.

## Ownership and compatibility

The weapon belongs to the shared crew stash and is compatible with [[Characters/Relay#c41-wire|Wire RELAY]]. Its fixed compatibility does not make it a Character-owned Machine Profile output.

## Acquisition and persistence

> **Accepted** — RELAY brings `EQ020` from M04 as authored starter gear and equips its default sickle form. M05 Research Terminal activation records its [[Machine Profiles/MP001|MP001 — Fabricator]] Output Blueprint as complete; owned Arc Cutter remains unequipped until selected or acquired in the field.

Later variants follow the [[Equipment/Overview#mission-acquisition-and-persistence|shared Blueprint acquisition and Equipment persistence rules]].
