## Role

> **Accepted** — Conductor Whip is Network's starter primary weapon: a directional melee fallback that supports rather than replaces her Controlled Units.

The starter variant conducts electricity through one flexible lash. It has RELAY's longest precise melee reach but lower direct damage, applying electrical status, strong Access buildup on compatible System Targets, and a brief **Command Mark** to the most recently struck valid target. Issuing Focus while that mark remains selects the target without separate targeting; marking does not issue an order or grant universal bonus damage. Death, expiry, invalidation, or leaving Command Range removes the mark. Later Network whip variants may use fire, poison, or other authored elements while retaining Command Mark.

A player may build around whip combat, but Network's strongest intended play remains coordination with Controlled Units. Whip attacks may reposition valid enemies but cannot grapple terrain, move RELAY through the environment, or create route access.

## Action mapping

The weapon responds to RELAY's abstract [[Gameplay/Actions#directional-attack|Directional Attack]] action. Physical bindings and exact directional resolution remain deferred.

## Resources

The whip has no ammunition. Exact cadence, reach, elemental buildup, damage, interruption, Focus interaction, and Access output require prototypes.

## Ownership and compatibility

The weapon belongs to the shared crew stash and is compatible with [[Characters/Relay#c42-network|Network RELAY]]. Element belongs to the individual Equipment item rather than changing through Machine Profile commands.

## Acquisition and persistence

> **Accepted** — RELAY brings `EQ021` from M04 as authored starter gear. It enters the stash when she joins and becomes selectable when Network unlocks; M05 Research Terminal activation records its [[Machine Profiles/MP002|MP002 — Line Runner]] Output Blueprint as complete.

Later variants follow the [[Equipment/Overview#mission-acquisition-and-persistence|shared Blueprint acquisition and Equipment persistence rules]].
