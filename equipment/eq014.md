## Role

> **Accepted** — The Minigun provides sustained suppression through a loaded magazine and increasing firing commitment.

[[Characters/Ram#c32|Siege RAM]] owns Heavy Firing anchoring and Breach interaction. This page owns ordinary fire, spin-up, magazine, reload, cadence, spread, and intrinsic projectile behaviour.

## Firing modes

| Mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Ordinary fire | Sustained fire while input remains active; movement and handling remain slower than lighter weapons |
| Heavy Firing | Siege anchors and commits to firing the full loaded magazine |

Heavy Firing cannot be cancelled freely and breaks RAM-reactive barriers through an explicit Siege modifier.

> **Accepted** — The Minigun uses a loaded magazine and reload cycle with unlimited reserve ammunition.
