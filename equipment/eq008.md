## Role

> **Accepted** — The Sniper Rifle trades firing frequency and positional stability for long-range impact.

[[Characters/Vector#c23|Hunter VECTOR]] owns its initial-loadout relationship and explicit Specialization modifiers. This page owns intrinsic firing, recovery, and recoil.

## Attack behaviour

| Property | Direction |
|---|---|
| Range | Long |
| Cadence | One deliberate shot followed by long recovery |
| Shooter response | Heavy kickback directly opposite the firing direction |
| Target response | Heavy knockback along the shot direction where target rules permit |
| Special ammunition | May fire the selected compatible Special Ammunition item |
| Wall Run | Cannot fire during Wall Run |

> **Accepted** — Recoil applies in all eight firing directions, including vertical and aerial shots. Hunter cannot steer during the initial impulse, regains ordinary air control afterward, and receives no invulnerability.

## Target constraints

Standard enemies may be displaced by a valid hit. Protected enemies and Bosses use authored knockback resistance or reduced displacement rather than guaranteed movement.

[[Characters/Vector#c23|Recoil Routing]] activates only when self-kickback reaches a valid wall, converting the collision into a Wall Run transition and shortening recovery. This is a Hunter modifier rather than an intrinsic Sniper Rifle rule; the rifle cannot fire during Wall Run.

## Resources

> **Accepted** — Ordinary Sniper Rifle rounds are unlimited. Long recovery and heavy self-kickback constrain standard fire. Special Ammunition owns its separate limited supply and effects.

The Sniper Rifle belongs to the shared crew stash. Hunter VECTOR owns its initial-loadout and compatibility relationship.
