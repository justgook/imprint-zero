## Role

> **Accepted** — Targeting Optics provide the information required for deliberate long-range shots without increasing the Sniper Rifle's intrinsic damage or reducing its recoil.

## Aiming information

| Feature | Function |
|---|---|
| Extended view | Shifts the readable view farther along Hunter's aim direction |
| Projected firing line | Shows the current shot path before firing |
| Weak-point information | Identifies authored weak points visible to the optics |
| Alignment information | Shows penetrable target relationships where the equipped round supports penetration |
