## Role

> **Accepted** — The Tower Shield blocks guarded attacks through directional cover rather than global invulnerability.

[[Characters/Ram#c31|Impact RAM]] owns Planted Guard, Shield Charge, Counterforce, and Counteroffensive modifiers. This page owns the shield's intrinsic guarding and Stability interaction.

## Guard states

| State | Movement | Ordinary guarded hit | Heavy guarded hit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile Guard | Slow movement; no jump while actively guarding | Blocked; consumes normal Stability | Blocked; consumes full authored Stability cost |
| Planted Guard | Impact is immobile and crouched behind the planted shield | Blocked; consumes no Stability | Blocked; consumes half its Mobile Guard Stability cost |

Rear, overhead, and otherwise unguarded attacks bypass the shield. Reaching zero Stability causes guard break and stagger.

## Impact interactions

- Stopping grounded movement automatically enters Planted Guard without a separate guard input.
- Moving, jumping, attacking, being displaced, or beginning Shield Charge exits Planted Guard.
- Shield Charge preserves forward protection but uses Mobile Guard Stability costs.
