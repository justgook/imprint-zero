## Role

> **Accepted** — Grounding Anchors prevent authored knockback from moving Siege RAM during Heavy Firing. They provide no invulnerability or passive damage mitigation.

## State interaction

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Ordinary movement or fire | Anchors remain inactive and provide no displacement resistance |
| Heavy Firing begins | Anchors deploy into the current valid surface |
| Heavy Firing active | Siege remains position-locked against authored knockback |
| Heavy Firing ends or is broken | Anchors retract before ordinary movement resumes |

Heavy attacks may still interrupt Heavy Firing where authored; preventing displacement does not guarantee action completion.

> **Accepted** — Grounding Anchors consume no charge, ammunition, or carried resource.
