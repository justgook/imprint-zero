## Role

> **Accepted** — Dorsal Tendrils are Null's starter primary weapon, using two thin back-mounted lashes for powerful directional melee attacks and Access setup.

The two tendrils may deliver simultaneous or alternating heavy lashes, broad sweeps, and piercing strikes while leaving RELAY's hands free for Hack presentation. They provide the highest direct impact of RELAY's starter weapons, build Access on compatible System Targets, but require the most committed recovery. Two-, four-, eight-, and other authored counts are separate `EQ###` primary items, and Null equips exactly one rig. Count changes attack identity rather than forming a linear quality ladder: two may favour heavy precision, four balanced coverage, and eight broad multi-target pressure with lower per-strike force or greater recovery.

A melee-heavy Null configuration is allowed as an alternative build, but it must not remove the intended value of target compatibility, Discovery Nodes, and prepared Hack Programs. Tendril hits build Access but never automatically execute an equipped program; Destructive Hack activation remains a separate deliberate gameplay verb. Tendrils cannot grapple terrain, move RELAY through the environment, or create route access.

## Action mapping

The weapon responds to RELAY's abstract [[Gameplay/Actions#directional-attack|Directional Attack]] action. Physical bindings and exact directional resolution remain deferred.

## Resources

The starter tendrils have no ammunition. Exact cadence, reach, recovery, simultaneous-target limits, damage, and Access buildup require prototypes.

## Ownership and compatibility

The weapon belongs to the shared crew stash and is compatible with [[Characters/Relay#c43-null|Null RELAY]]. It is physical Equipment rather than a Hack Program or Wire Integration.

## Acquisition and persistence

> **Accepted** — RELAY brings `EQ022` from M04 as authored starter gear. It enters the stash when she joins and becomes selectable when Null unlocks; M05 Research Terminal activation records its [[Machine Profiles/MP003|MP003 — Process Warden]] Output Blueprint as complete.

Later variants follow the [[Equipment/Overview#mission-acquisition-and-persistence|shared Blueprint acquisition and Equipment persistence rules]].
