## Role

> **Accepted** — A Resonance Beacon Round exchanges ordinary rifle impact for local enemy control.

It is Hunter VECTOR's initial Special Ammunition item. Future ammunition discoveries replace it in the same loadout slot rather than consuming additional slots.

## Impact behaviour

| Impact | Result |
|---|---|
| Valid enemy | Embeds a temporary resonance beacon in the target with negligible direct damage |
| Valid surface | Embeds a temporary resonance beacon at the impact point |
| Standard enemy inside pulse radius | Briefly stunned or interrupted |
| Enemy outside pulse radius but inside hearing range | Enters [[Gameplay/Mechanics#enemy-awareness|Searching]] and investigates the beacon without automatically Tracking Hunter |
| Protected enemy or Boss | Resists the stun or receives a reduced effect but still hears the beacon |
