## Role

> **Accepted** — The Kinetic Stabilizer supplies rechargeable internal Stability to compatible shields without providing passive damage mitigation on its own.

## Stability

| Event | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Valid guarded hit | Shield consumes its authored Stability cost |
| Stability reaches zero | Compatible shield guard breaks and RAM is staggered |
| Shield is not absorbing impacts | Stability recharges under unresolved timing rules |

Future compatible stabilizers may vary capacity, recharge, heavy-impact resistance, or guard-break recovery without changing the Tower Shield's identity.
