## Role

> **Accepted** — Climbing Claws extend Ghost VECTOR's surface control from shared Wall Run into sustained wall and ceiling climbing.

The Claws do not create a separate Character route language. Every VECTOR Specialization retains [[Characters/Vector#shared-movement|Wall Run]]; Ghost uses this Equipment to approach those spaces more slowly and deliberately.

## Surface behaviour

| Surface | Capability |
|---|---|
| Valid wall | Transition from Wall Run into sustained attachment; stop, reverse, hold, or climb |
| Valid ceiling | Transfer onto the ceiling, attach, and move along it |
| Invalid surface | No attachment |

> **Accepted** — Climbing Claws consume no charge, stamina, or carried resource.

> **Accepted** — Ghost may throw or perform a contextual Kunai melee strike while attached.
