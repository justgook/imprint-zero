## Role

> **Accepted** — The Phase Shifter enables Phase Shift by temporarily moving VECTOR onto an adjacent phase layer where ordinary attacks cannot interact with her.

The Equipment is the **Phase Shifter**; its activated action is **Phase Shift**; its resource units are **Phase Charges**.

## Phase Shift

| Property | Rule |
|---|---|
| Direction | Eight-directional |
| Movement | Fixed, committed dash with no steering after activation |
| Cost | One Phase Charge |
| Recovery | Spent charges regenerate sequentially after a cooldown |
| Protection | Invulnerable during the authored phase window |
| Attack traversal | Passes through ordinary projectiles, attacks, hazards, and enemy bodies |
| World geometry | Cannot pass through solid terrain, locked gates, or route barriers; stops at the last valid position |
| Route boundary | Combat and evasion action, not a separate optional-route key |

## Variants

Future Phase Shifter Equipment may vary:

- maximum Phase Charges;
- shift distance;
- invulnerability duration;
- recharge behaviour.

Each accepted variant receives its own stable Equipment ID rather than changing `EQ006`'s identity. Ordinary weapon hits do not restore Phase Charges; [[Characters/Vector#c22|Phase Cascade]] provides the exceptional combo-finisher restoration.

## Ownership and compatibility

The Phase Shifter belongs to the shared crew stash. [[Characters/Vector#c22|Phase VECTOR]] owns its initial-loadout relationship and the ability to cancel compatible weapon attacks into Phase Shift.

## Acquisition and persistence

> **Accepted** — `EQ006` enters the crew stash when C22 Phase unlocks.

The Phase Shifter then follows the [[Equipment/Overview#mission-acquisition-and-persistence|shared Equipment persistence rules]].
