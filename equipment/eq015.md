## Role

> **Accepted** — Proximity Mines create visible local threat zones around a committed Siege position.

They support anchoring rather than replacing direct weapon fire or creating invisible unavoidable damage.

## Behaviour

| Event | Result |
|---|---|
| Placement | Deploy one mine on a valid nearby surface |
| Valid enemy enters trigger region | Mine signals activation, then detonates after an authored warning window |
| Invalid surface or occupied limit | Placement fails with readable feedback and no silent resource loss |

> **Accepted** — Proximity Mines use a limited carried supply and must remain visually readable to the player.
