## Attack and counterplay

> **TODO — Behaviour review:** Validate movement, facing, commitment, collision, reach, interruption, vulnerable/protected states and tuning with introductory RAM. These cycles are proposals, not inherited approval from the Controlled Unit's behaviour.

| Element | Proposed behaviour |
|---|---|
| Cycle | Pause and face the target, visibly brace the reinforced front, commit to a grounded horizontal ram without turning or jumping, then brake/recover. No tracking after commitment or passive body-contact damage is proposed. |
| Counterplay | Use ordinary movement/jump or an authored safe lane to avoid the commitment, then attack during braking. Frontal guard/charge collision uses Equipment and actor resistance rules, not automatic invulnerability or guaranteed displacement. |
| Placement | Provide readable run-up and braking room. Do not start hidden off-screen rams, trap RAM in a corridor narrower than his evasion options, or stack a cutter and security pulse on the same commitment. |

Damage, Integrity and explicit modifiers follow [[Gameplay/Gameplay Math|shared math]]. No numeric actor inputs are assigned yet. A machine's hostile configuration and later Controlled Unit configuration need a reviewed relationship; neither silently replaces the other's owner.
