## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — power distribution and contact servicing.** Electrical contact servicing becomes a charged, short committed strike; read charge and spacing rather than equip a counter-element. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

**Tiny swarm worker:** [[Biomes/B03#micro-swarm-scale-and-surface-movement|B03's shared micro-scale and surface-movement rules]] apply. Individual units are light threats; collective arrival, lane pressure and tool mixtures supply danger. They can enter from shelf gaps, walls and ceiling undersides as well as the floor, without becoming permanent unreachable enemies.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Stops, visibly charges and commits its electrode to one bounded short lane. A visible cable may establish a separately authored connection, never an invisible proximity network. |
| Counterplay | Charge and discharged recovery are readable amid the swarm. Ordinary attacks engage the whole tiny body; no electrical immunity, compulsory Hack or precision electrode shot. |
| Combinations | Stagger charge against Skitter rushes and reserve a reachable approach. Any later connected endpoint needs its own tell and attack owner; a wire alone causes no extra attack. |
| Defeat | Disable this hostile body for the current deployment; no automatic data deletion or Mission-control operation. |

Finite swarms use staggered commitments, safe waiting/withdrawal space and ordinary-kit reachable engagement for all Characters and unlocked Specializations. Validate low-body hit intersections and descending approaches rather than assuming a standing-height socket; a ceiling-only survivor must never softlock the local clear. No compulsory Hack, Overdrive, immunity, wall pursuit or high jump belongs to this definition. ## System and succession boundaries

The authored machine body is a Powered System. A Connected System relationship requires an explicit visible compatible connection; proximity or shared Biome is insufficient. Field Access/Hack and Cascade Virus retain their shared owners. No Machine Profile, Blueprint drop table, Controlled Unit, Wire Integration or Program grant is allocated.

Any helper archetype can physically integrate its complete small body into the larger Curator host and supply its controller role; this is not an interchangeable large-machine component. Reclaimers reconstruct the body; succession is a separate protected post-Boss event, not the individual enemy's combat defeat effect, an automatic elemental Boss variant or proof of mind continuity.
