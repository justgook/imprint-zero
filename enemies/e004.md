## Tactical role

> **Accepted** — A grounded mobile containment machine that prevents stationary waiting from solving every encounter and teaches committed attack timing. First authored for [[Missions/M02|M02]]; the Mission owns placement, pursuit boundaries, and combinations.

## Attack cycle

> **Accepted** — Approach, stop within short range, visibly wind up, strike forward once, then visibly recover before moving again. Facing and attack position lock during wind-up; no turning or chasing mid-strike. No leap, grab, ranged attack, or body-contact damage.

Both Characters can escape with ordinary movement. Crossing behind rewards positioning but does not cancel the committed forward strike or recovery.

## Collision and vulnerability

> **Accepted** — The body does not block Character passage or deal contact damage; crossing requires no Phase Shift or other Character-specific move. The active strike remains damaging.

Vulnerable throughout the cycle from either side, without shields, rear-only weak points, or recovery-only damage gates. Recovery is the safest counterattack opportunity, not the only damage window. Ordinary hits do not automatically cancel the committed strike; explicit stagger and stun effects remain separate under shared combat rules.

## Visual identity

![Containment patrol silhouette and striking-arm study](content/images/enemies/e004.svg)

> **Accepted** — Low wheeled hospital-security machine with a clearly visible forward striking arm, not a humanoid guard or medical worker. Maintained but worn institutional hardware; attack parts remain readable at gameplay scale. The image is a placeholder, not approved art.

## Placement and rewards

[[Missions/M02|M02]] introduces this role before combining it with [[Enemies/E003|E003]]. Detection does not pass through walls; M02 authors room-bounded pursuit and return behaviour. Required encounters permit ordinary evasion, including crossing the non-blocking body. M02 has no enemy loot or respawning. No Machine Profile is required merely because the enemy is a machine; later research or RELAY use needs its own decision.
