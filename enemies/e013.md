## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — welding and controlled disposal.** A welding nozzle makes a narrow flame sweep; choose when to leave its working lane and approach during cooldown. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

| Element | Working behaviour |
