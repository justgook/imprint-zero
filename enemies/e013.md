## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — heavy cartridge handling.** Heavy lifting becomes a slower, stronger close-range clamp strike, distinct from Skitter's rush. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Walks at ground height, plants its feet, spreads the clamps visibly and commits to one short strike. It does not grab and scramble player controls. |
| Counterplay | The wide wind-up permits withdrawal; after the strike the front handling socket is exposed. Protection is not blanket invulnerability. |
| Combinations | Provide standing attack access and room to withdraw for RAM. A Gripper commitment cannot coincide with a specialist covering its entire escape area. |
| Defeat | Disable this hostile body for the current deployment; no automatic data deletion or Mission-control operation. |

Finite batches use staggered commitments, safe waiting/withdrawal space and standing-height targets for all Characters and unlocked Specializations. No compulsory Hack, Overdrive, immunity, wall pursuit or high jump belongs to this definition.
