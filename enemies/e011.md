## Tactical role

> **Working version — B03 archive guard:** A grounded recording/security machine whose visible shutter cycle alternates lane pressure with a reachable frontal opening. It asks the player to leave a committed firing lane and punish recovery, not to bring a particular Character or Hack configuration. [[Missions/MA01#encounter-staging|MA01]] owns its first fully authored Act II placements.

This is the **one shared B03 archetype** reserved for MA01/MA02 and the ROOK/RELAY finishes in [[Missions/M05#encounter-staging|M05]]. MA02's encounters and M05's additional counts/activation choreography remain unauthored; those references do not allocate variants or establish that all archive machines originated at Helix.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Movement | Slow grounded indexing within its local lane; stops before targeting. No flight, ceiling position, diagonal bombardment or pursuit across safe-room boundaries. |
| Observation | A visible recording lens and harmless narrow scan identify facing. Armoured shutters reduce incoming frontal damage; this is not blanket invulnerability or a rear-only puzzle. |
| Commitment | A distinct mechanical wind-up locks facing/aim, followed by one horizontal security burst. No instantaneous retarget during the burst; the scan itself deals no damage. |
| Recovery | Shutters open and the front assembly remains vulnerable at ordinary standing/melee height long enough for an approach and ordinary attack. No Hack, Overdrive or specific Equipment is required. |
| Counterplay | Leave the committed lane, use available cover or a compatible directional guard, then exploit recovery. Telegraphed shots must be avoidable with the slowest ordinary kit, not only VECTOR's mobility. |
| Combinations | Start singly, then stagger two cycles. A larger room may place a third unit behind a separate approach, never three simultaneous unavoidable crossing lanes. |
| Defeat | Disable the individual hostile body and its recording indicators; this neither deletes stored archive records nor operates required Mission controls. |

## System and research boundaries

The machine is a **Powered System**; an explicitly linked pair can also supply **Connected System** targets for [[Machine Profiles/NH003#constraints|Cascade Virus]]. Its damage-over-time and one-hop transmission affect compatible hostile bodies, not required route infrastructure or the broadcast package. A visible cable establishes a transmission relationship; mere proximity or shared Biome does not connect every guard. The memory in [[Imprints/Relay Null#cutscenes|RELAY Null]] may depict this body without proving where or when the memory occurred.

Field Hack, Access and Program Execution follow their shared owners. The main route remains combat-resolvable by every configuration. No Controlled Unit, Wire Integration, machine result, loot track or Hack-board reward is allocated by declaring these system tags.
