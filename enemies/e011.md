## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — small-item transport.** Small-item handling becomes basic close pressure: read the rush, step out and punish its stop. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Ground-level approach, visible brace and one short committed rush/clamp strike. No homing during commitment, chain leap or ceiling-only hit area. |
| Counterplay | Attacks expose a reachable frontal socket during recovery. Ordinary melee or ranged attacks can punish it; no rear-only requirement. |
| Combinations | Stagger pairs; never use the whole batch to seal every retreat. No cross-room pursuit or collision pile-up around a safe control. |
| Defeat | Disable this hostile body for the current deployment; no automatic data deletion or Mission-control operation. |

Finite batches use staggered commitments, safe waiting/withdrawal space and standing-height targets for all Characters and unlocked Specializations. No compulsory Hack, Overdrive, immunity, wall pursuit or high jump belongs to this definition. ## System and succession boundaries

The authored machine body is a Powered System. A Connected System relationship requires an explicit visible compatible connection; proximity or shared Biome is insufficient. Field Access/Hack and Cascade Virus retain their shared owners. No Machine Profile, Blueprint drop table, Controlled Unit, Wire Integration or Program grant is allocated.

Any helper archetype can supply the replacement Curator controller. Reclaimers reconstruct the body; succession is a separate protected post-Boss event, not the individual enemy's combat defeat effect, an automatic elemental Boss variant or proof of mind continuity. [[Bosses/BS004#succession-and-later-visits|BS004]] owns that sequence and later-visit hostility.

Colour is a secondary cue: tool shape, motion, commitment and sound must distinguish this role without colour vision.
