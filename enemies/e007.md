## Tactical role

> **Working version — M03 roster:** Grounded shield-bearing gang combatant; forces a choice between frontal pressure and repositioning. This is the current behaviour baseline for [[Missions/M03|M03's gang base]]. M03 owns faction naming, counts, local clear requirements, and placement.
