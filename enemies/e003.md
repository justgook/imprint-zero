## Tactical role

> **Accepted** — A stationary containment machine that teaches timing and crossing a watched horizontal lane. First authored for [[Missions/M02|M02]]; the Mission owns placement and combinations.

## Attack cycle

> **Accepted** — Detect the Character in the firing lane, signal targeting with a visible lamp and rising audio cue, lock aim, fire one straight projectile, then visibly cool down before targeting again. No tracking after aim lock, sweeping beam, or instant contact damage.

Where mounting permits, select left or right before aim lock. Commit through the shot and cooldown; turning resumes only afterward. Crossing behind after lock does not cause retargeting. Wall placement may restrict the unit to one direction.

## Mounting and vulnerability

> **Accepted** — Floor, wall, and ceiling mounting all retain horizontal fire along the gameplay plane. Ceiling units need a lowered firing assembly that threatens the lane while preserving ordinary evasion. No vertical or diagonal aiming.

The exposed firing assembly is vulnerable throughout the cycle. Mounting hardware is scenery, not armour; no shield or cooldown-only damage gate. Cooldown is the safest counterattack opportunity, not the only one. Both deployed Characters must be able to reach the assembly with available baseline attacks.
