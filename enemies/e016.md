## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — physical wire-web installation and repair.** Service work becomes interruptible support through existing machinery, not a generic spider bite or a global buff. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

**Tiny swarm worker:** [[Biomes/B03#micro-swarm-scale-and-surface-movement|B03's shared micro-scale and surface-movement rules]] apply. Individual units are light threats; collective arrival, lane pressure and tool mixtures supply danger. They can enter from shelf gaps, walls and ceiling undersides as well as the floor, without becoming permanent unreachable enemies.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Establishes one visible local connection to an already-present compatible spider or authored security endpoint, then coordinates its ordinary defensive cycle. No instant new shot, hidden shortened tell or universal shield. |
| Counterplay | Interrupt the reachable connector/body or handle its recipient. Surface travel must not leave the support permanently ceiling-only; connection loss ends coordination and a new connection needs visible wind-up. |
| Combinations | Baseline attacks must reach the connector/body without ceiling pursuit. No network-wide buff, endless spawning or killed-enemy reconstruction; recipient attacks remain with their own Enemy page. |
| Defeat | Disable this hostile body for the current deployment; no automatic data deletion or Mission-control operation. |

Finite swarms use staggered commitments, safe waiting/withdrawal space and ordinary-kit reachable engagement for all Characters and unlocked Specializations. Validate low-body hit intersections and descending approaches rather than assuming a standing-height socket; a ceiling-only survivor must never softlock the local clear. No compulsory Hack, Overdrive, immunity, wall pursuit or high jump belongs to this definition. [[Gameplay/Gameplay Math|Shared math]] owns damage calculation; numerical damage, Integrity, protection, speed, reach and timing are tuning TODOs.

MA02's connection/service encounter owns its first direction; linked recipients, room IDs and quantities remain unauthored. These are local encounter directions, not measured geometry, automatic random spawns or a claim that all seven spiders occur in every Mission.

## System and succession boundaries

The authored machine body is a Powered System. A Connected System relationship requires an explicit visible compatible connection; proximity or shared Biome is insufficient. Field Access/Hack and Cascade Virus retain their shared owners. No Machine Profile, Blueprint drop table, Controlled Unit, Wire Integration or Program grant is allocated.

Any helper archetype can physically integrate its complete small body into the larger Curator host and supply its controller role; this is not an interchangeable large-machine component. Reclaimers reconstruct the body; succession is a separate protected post-Boss event, not the individual enemy's combat defeat effect, an automatic elemental Boss variant or proof of mind continuity. [[Bosses/BS004#succession-and-later-visits|BS004]] owns that sequence and later-visit hostility.

## Visual, audio and validation

Muted blue-green tool marks; conspicuous spool, plug and anchor grippers. Colour is a secondary cue: tool shape, motion, commitment and sound must distinguish this role without colour vision. No final sprite, leg count, generated image, status buildup or immunity matrix is allocated.
