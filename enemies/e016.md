## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — power distribution and contact servicing.** Electrical contact servicing becomes a charged, short committed strike; read charge and spacing rather than equip a counter-element. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Stops, visibly charges and commits its electrode to one bounded short lane. A visible cable may establish a separately authored connection, never an invisible proximity network. |
| Counterplay | Charge and discharged recovery leave a standing-height frontal target. Ordinary withdrawal and attacks work; no compulsory Hack or electrical immunity. |
| Combinations | Stagger charge against Skitter rushes and reserve a reachable approach. Any later connected endpoint needs its own tell and attack owner; a wire alone causes no extra attack. |
| Defeat | Disable this hostile body for the current deployment; no automatic data deletion or Mission-control operation. |

Finite batches use staggered commitments, safe waiting/withdrawal space and standing-height targets for all Characters and unlocked Specializations. No compulsory Hack, Overdrive, immunity, wall pursuit or high jump belongs to this definition. [[Gameplay/Gameplay Math|Shared math]] owns damage calculation; numerical damage, Integrity, protection, speed, reach and timing are tuning TODOs.

## Mission handoff

MA01 R07 mixes one with two Skitters. Later linked-machine combinations belong to their local Mission authoring. These are local encounter directions, not measured geometry, automatic random spawns or a claim that all seven spiders occur in every Mission.

## System and succession boundaries

The authored machine body is a Powered System. A Connected System relationship requires an explicit visible compatible connection; proximity or shared Biome is insufficient. Field Access/Hack and Cascade Virus retain their shared owners. No Machine Profile, Blueprint drop table, Controlled Unit, Wire Integration or Program grant is allocated.

Any helper archetype can supply the replacement Curator controller. Reclaimers reconstruct the body; succession is a separate protected post-Boss event, not the individual enemy's combat defeat effect, an automatic elemental Boss variant or proof of mind continuity. [[Bosses/BS004#succession-and-later-visits|BS004]] owns that sequence and later-visit hostility.

## Visual, audio and validation

Muted amber-yellow tool marks; exposed electrode fork, charging coil and distinct crackling cue. Colour is a secondary cue: tool shape, motion, commitment and sound must distinguish this role without colour vision. No final sprite, leg count, generated image, status buildup or immunity matrix is allocated.
