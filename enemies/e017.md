## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — wreckage recovery and body reconstruction.** Salvage handling supplies load-bearing close pressure; its reconstruction task is separate from combat resurrection. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Carries a bounded salvage load at ground height, braces and makes one short hauling-clamp strike. It cannot revive cleared enemies or endlessly manufacture replacements during combat. |
| Counterplay | Commitment/recovery lowers the load and exposes its frontal handling socket. The load is not a permanent rear-only shield; every kit can attack the body. |
| Combinations | Combat cargo never contains compulsory destruction of victim records. Post-defeat Reclaimers arrive in a protected succession sequence, not another hostile wave or a requirement to spare one combat unit. |
| Defeat | Disable this hostile body for the current deployment; no automatic data deletion or Mission-control operation. |

Finite batches use staggered commitments, safe waiting/withdrawal space and standing-height targets for all Characters and unlocked Specializations. No compulsory Hack, Overdrive, immunity, wall pursuit or high jump belongs to this definition. [[Gameplay/Gameplay Math|Shared math]] owns damage calculation; numerical damage, Integrity, protection, speed, reach and timing are tuning TODOs.

## Mission handoff

MA03's salvage/service approach owns its first hostile encounter direction, with no room IDs/counts yet. BS004 owns its separate post-defeat reconstruction presentation. ## System and succession boundaries

The authored machine body is a Powered System. A Connected System relationship requires an explicit visible compatible connection; proximity or shared Biome is insufficient. Field Access/Hack and Cascade Virus retain their shared owners. No Machine Profile, Blueprint drop table, Controlled Unit, Wire Integration or Program grant is allocated.

Any helper archetype can supply the replacement Curator controller. Reclaimers reconstruct the body; succession is a separate protected post-Boss event, not the individual enemy's combat defeat effect, an automatic elemental Boss variant or proof of mind continuity. [[Bosses/BS004#succession-and-later-visits|BS004]] owns that sequence and later-visit hostility.

## Visual, audio and validation

Dirty chalk-white tool marks; salvage basket, detachable cargo clamp and repair manipulators. Colour is a secondary cue: tool shape, motion, commitment and sound must distinguish this role without colour vision. No final sprite, leg count, generated image, status buildup or immunity matrix is allocated.
