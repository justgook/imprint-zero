## Tactical role

**B03 service spider — welding and controlled disposal.** A welding nozzle makes a narrow flame sweep; choose when to leave its working lane and approach during cooldown. The compact mechanical helper shares the Curator's articulated work-limb vocabulary, not its human torso or an established biological species. [[Biomes/B03#service-spider-roster|B03]] owns the seven-role ecology.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Working behaviour |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Stops, ignites visibly/audibly and sweeps one bounded short lane without tracking the player through the sweep. No permanent arena-wide burning floor. |
| Counterplay | Nozzle cooldown exposes the reachable front assembly. Leaving the lane and ordinary attacks suffice; no fire-resistant kit is required. |
| Combinations | Keep a safe withdrawal space outside the flame envelope for the slowest kit. Skitters cannot force the player into an unavoidable simultaneous sweep. |
| Defeat | Disable this hostile body for the current deployment; no automatic data deletion or Mission-control operation. |

Finite batches use staggered commitments, safe waiting/withdrawal space and standing-height targets for all Characters and unlocked Specializations. No compulsory Hack, Overdrive, immunity, wall pursuit or high jump belongs to this definition. [[Gameplay/Gameplay Math|Shared math]] owns damage calculation; numerical damage, Integrity, protection, speed, reach and timing are tuning TODOs.

## Mission handoff

MA01 R05 pairs one with a Skitter; later MA02 disposal/service encounters remain unquantified. These are local encounter directions, not measured geometry, automatic random spawns or a claim that all seven spiders occur in every Mission.

## System and succession boundaries

The authored machine body is a Powered System. A Connected System relationship requires an explicit visible compatible connection; proximity or shared Biome is insufficient. Field Access/Hack and Cascade Virus retain their shared owners. No Machine Profile, Blueprint drop table, Controlled Unit, Wire Integration or Program grant is allocated.

Any helper archetype can supply the replacement Curator controller. Reclaimers reconstruct the body; succession is a separate protected post-Boss event, not the individual enemy's combat defeat effect, an automatic elemental Boss variant or proof of mind continuity. [[Bosses/BS004#succession-and-later-visits|BS004]] owns that sequence and later-visit hostility.

## Visual, audio and validation

Faded orange tool marks; short welding nozzle, heat shield and audible ignition. Colour is a secondary cue: tool shape, motion, commitment and sound must distinguish this role without colour vision. No final sprite, leg count, generated image, status buildup or immunity matrix is allocated.
