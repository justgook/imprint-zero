## Tactical role

> **Proposal — M03 roster:** Grounded ranged gang combatant; teaches leaving an exposed lane and using freight cover. Authored for [[Missions/M03|M03's gang base]], not yet accepted behaviour. M03 owns faction naming, counts, local clear requirements, and placement.

## Attack and counterplay

| Element | Proposed behaviour |
|---|---|
| Cycle | Stop, face the Character, visibly raise a short firearm, commit to a horizontal firing direction, fire a short burst, then visibly reload. Proposed: no tracking after commitment, diagonal aiming, or shots through structural cover. |
| Counterplay | Use cover or cross the committed lane, then close distance during reload. Do not require long-range fire: VECTOR's baseline attacks must reach the combatant. |
| Placement | M03 introduces this role before mixing it with E006's approach pressure. Stagger placement so ranged enemies do not all start their first burst together. |
